## Threads in the nucleus

Inside the nucleus of a cell are **chromosomes**, thread-like structures that carry genetic information. This cell is drawn with only four of them, so that you can count.

<!-- STEP -->
## Each one is copied

Before the cell divides, every chromosome is copied. Each copy stays beside its original.

<!-- STEP -->
## The copies move apart

The copies are pulled to opposite ends, and the cell begins to pinch in the middle.

<!-- STEP -->
## Two daughter cells

**Mitosis is a cell division that produces two daughter cells, each with the same number of chromosomes as the parent cell.**

<!-- STEP -->
## Clones

In all the organisms you have studied so far, mitosis is the central process behind asexual reproduction. The offspring are genetically identical to the parent, and they are called **clones**.
