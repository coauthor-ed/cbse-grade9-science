## One way of copying

A yeast bud, a hydra bud, a spore and a rose cutting look very different. Yet in each, mitosis gives new cells with the same chromosomes, so the offspring are clones.

<!-- STEP -->
## Fast when conditions are good

Copying by mitosis is fast. It helps organisms increase quickly, especially when conditions are favourable. Press **One round of copying** to watch the numbers grow.

## Half an answer

Why do simple organisms like yeast and hydra mainly reproduce asexually? Part of the answer is here: in good conditions, one parent can quickly fill a place with copies of itself.

**But copies are all alike, so where do differences come from?** Sexual reproduction comes next.
