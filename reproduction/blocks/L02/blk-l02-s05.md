# Three Days on the Counter

Here is the thing about mould you already know now, whether or not you have put it together: the spores were in the air of this room before you started reading.

You cannot remove them, and you cannot out-wait them. You can only decide what the tray of cut fruit sits on, whether it is covered, whether it is wet or dry, and whether one piece is out or all of them are.

You have three days. Get the fruit through all three with nothing visible on it, and keep every piece you like - not just enough to look as though you managed.

Set the conditions, start the day, and watch the growth counter run. Change your mind as often as you like; there is no clock on this one.
