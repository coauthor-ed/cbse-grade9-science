## An old idea

For a long time, people thought living things arose on their own from non-living matter, because fuzz seems to appear on old food from nowhere.

<!-- STEP -->
## Life comes from life

Experiments by Louis Pasteur proved that new life always comes from pre-existing life. His germ theory of disease also led to the sterilising of food and instruments, and he strengthened the cell theory: all cells arise from pre-existing cells.

<!-- STEP -->
## Why a refrigerator helps

Lower temperatures slow or stop the reproduction of moulds and bacteria. Choose a place for the food and compare.

Before refrigerators became common about 100 years ago, fresh food lasted only 1-2 days.
