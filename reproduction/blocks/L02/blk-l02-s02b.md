## More than spoiled food

Moulds on bread may look unpleasant, but fungi benefit society greatly. They grow fast and break down organic wastes and pollutants, and they help remove heavy metals from industrial wastes.

## Antibiotics

Many antibiotics, such as penicillin, come from fungi. These have saved countless lives from bacterial infections.

Do you know any fungus that can degrade plastic? Find out and share it with your class.
