# Pollinators Around You

Apple yields in the lower Himalayan region are falling, and this is linked with climate change and fewer natural pollinators. Why might pollinators be declining near you, and what could help them?

Take this outside the classroom. There is no single right answer. These points may help you begin:

- Visit crop fields near your school. Ask farmers which pollinators visit each crop, and why their numbers are falling.
- Ask which crops they grow from seed and which by vegetative propagation. Do they use indigenous or hybrid seeds, and why?
- Watch your school garden. Record which pollinators visit which plants, and discuss what you find in class.
