# What Can a Flower Tell Us?

<!-- STEP -->
## Tomato

In a tomato flower, the stamens cover the stigma. Pollen from those stamens has a very short way to go, so this flower has probably been **self-pollinated**.

<!-- STEP -->
## Wheat

Wheat flowers open only after pollination. Wheat is wind-pollinated, yet by the time a flower opens its own pollen has already reached its stigma, so it is mostly self-pollinated.

<!-- STEP -->
## Papaya

Male and female flowers are often borne on different papaya trees. A female flower on such a tree has no pollen on its own tree, so it needs **cross-pollination**.

**A flower's shape and timing give good clues to how pollen reaches it. They are clues, so we say "might have been", not "must have been".**
