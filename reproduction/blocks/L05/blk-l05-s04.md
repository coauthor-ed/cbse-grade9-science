# From Stigma to Egg Cell

<!-- STEP -->
## Pollen on the stigma

A pollen grain has landed on the stigma of a china-rose flower, which is also called hibiscus or gudhal. Pollination is now complete, but the male gamete is still far from the egg cell. **Which process is about to happen next?**

<!-- STEP -->
## A tube grows

The grain produces a **pollen tube**, which grows down through the style.

<!-- STEP -->
## Down into the ovary

The tube grows into the ovary, and the male gamete moves through it towards an ovule.

<!-- STEP -->
## The gametes fuse

Inside the ovule, the male gamete fuses with the egg cell. This fusion is **fertilisation**, and it marks the beginning of a new life.

<!-- STEP -->
## Zygote and embryo

The fertilised egg is called a **zygote**, and it later develops into an **embryo**.

<!-- STEP -->
## If the tube is blocked

Pollination and fertilisation are different events. Here the style is blocked. The grain still lands and the tube still starts, but the male gamete cannot reach the egg cell, so no zygote forms.

**A pollinated flower is not always a fertilised flower.**
