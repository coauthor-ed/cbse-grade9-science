# From Flower to Fruit

<!-- STEP -->
## The ovary becomes the fruit

While the embryo develops, the ovary around the ovules enlarges and develops into a **fruit**. Look at this tomato flower and the fruit that follows it.

<!-- STEP -->
## The ovules become seeds

The ovules develop into **seeds** inside the fruit. Cut a tomato in the kitchen and draw what you see, both lengthwise and across.

<!-- STEP -->
## Seeds travel

Seed dispersal takes place by wind, water and animals. Madar and dandelion seeds carry fine, fluffy hairs. The breeze carries them away from the parent plant.

<!-- STEP -->
## A new plant

When water, air and temperature are favourable, a seed germinates and grows into a new plant.

**Sexual reproduction gives new plants and also variation, which helps plant species survive and adapt.**
