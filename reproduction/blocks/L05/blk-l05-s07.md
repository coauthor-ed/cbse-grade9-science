# Pollination on the Farm

<!-- STEP -->
## Selective breeding

Sexual reproduction in plants matters in farming. In **selective breeding**, farmers select plants with the characters they want, and those plants are used for reproduction.

<!-- STEP -->
## Artificial hybridisation

**Artificial hybridisation** uses steps you have already seen. The stamens are removed, the flower is covered with a bag to prevent self-pollination, and pollen with the wanted characters is transferred by hand.

<!-- STEP -->
## Genetic engineering

In **genetic engineering**, the genetic material for a wanted character is inserted into the DNA of a chosen variety. These methods give new varieties, such as high yielding and disease resistant ones.

<!-- STEP -->
## A scientist of embryology

P. Maheshwari is known as the Father of Indian Embryology. He developed in-vitro fertilisation in flowering plants by fusing an egg and a male gamete in a test tube, which created new hybrid plants. He was also among the first to grow plant embryos on artificial nutrient media.
