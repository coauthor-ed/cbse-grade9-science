## Two Ways to Use Pollen

Here are rough figures from studies of flowers. A wind-pollinated grass such as maize releases **5,00,000 to 10,00,000** pollen grains, and **50 to 200** seeds form. An insect-pollinated plant such as sunflower releases **20,000 to 40,000** grains, and **800 to 1,000** seeds form.

**Predict:** Which plant uses less pollen for each seed?
