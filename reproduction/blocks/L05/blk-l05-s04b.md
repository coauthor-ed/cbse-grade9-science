# Testing Pollen in Sugar

Suresh wants to study how pollen germinates, which means how a grain starts its tube. On microscope slides he puts pollen grains in sugar solutions of 0%, 2.5%, 5%, 7.5% and 10%.

Think this through and write your answers. There is no single right answer. These questions may help you begin:

- Sugar concentration is the one thing Suresh changes. What hypotheses could he test with this set-up?
- What should stay the same on every slide, so that any difference comes from the sugar?
