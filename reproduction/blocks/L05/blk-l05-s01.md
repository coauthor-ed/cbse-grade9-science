# Whose Pollen Reached the Stigma?

<!-- STEP -->
## The same flower

Pollination can happen within one flower. Pollen from an anther lands on the stigma of that same flower, and this is called **self-pollination**.

<!-- STEP -->
## Another flower, same plant

Pollen may instead land on the stigma of another flower on the same plant. This is self-pollination too, because the pollen has not left its own plant.

<!-- STEP -->
## Another plant of the same type

When pollen goes from a flower of one plant to the stigma of a flower of another plant of the same type, it is **cross-pollination**.

**The word depends on which plant the pollen came from, not on how far it went.**
