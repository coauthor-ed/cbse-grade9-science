# What does it mean to be sexually mature?

<!-- STEP -->
## A body that can reproduce

As we grow through adolescence, our bodies undergo many changes. A significant part of this growth is **sexual maturation**, which means the body is becoming capable of reproduction.

The production of sperm in boys and the menstrual cycle in girls are signs of it. These changes come gradually, and they start at different ages in different people.

<!-- STEP -->
## Emotional maturity takes longer

A person can look grown up and still not be ready for adult responsibilities, because emotional maturity takes longer than sexual maturity.

Being emotionally mature means three things:

- handling your feelings
- communicating clearly
- making thoughtful decisions

**A body that can reproduce does not mean a person who is ready.**
