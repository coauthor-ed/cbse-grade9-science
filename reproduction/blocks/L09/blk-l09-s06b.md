# Why are human babies dependent for so long?

In many animals, the young ones can walk or find food soon after birth, but human babies are completely dependent on adults for a long time.

What might be some advantages and disadvantages of this for humans as a species? There is no single right answer. These thoughts may help you begin:

- A long childhood gives more time to learn language and skills from others.
- A helpless baby needs care for years, and this asks a lot of a family.
- Think about how a family or a village shares that work.
