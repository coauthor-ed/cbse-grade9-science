# Why does the law ban finding out a baby's sex?

<!-- STEP -->
## Abortion

In some cases, an unwanted pregnancy can be removed by surgery, generally only within the first trimester, when the embryo is very small. This procedure is called **abortion**. The law allows it only under set conditions, and only with a qualified doctor.

<!-- STEP -->
## Sex ratio and the law

The practice of self-selective abortion is a significant concern, often driven by a preference for a specific gender. It can lead to a marked imbalance in the sex ratio of a society.

**So prenatal sex determination is strictly prohibited by law in India.**

This law is important for keeping a balanced sex ratio in a healthy, forward-looking society.
