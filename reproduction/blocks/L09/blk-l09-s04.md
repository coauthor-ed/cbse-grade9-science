# What are sexually transmitted infections?

<!-- STEP -->
## The risk that remains

Infections remain a risk for that couple. Pills work on the release of eggs, so they do nothing to stop an infection passing between two people.

Sexual activity involves close physical contact, so some infections can pass from an infected person to an uninfected person. These are called **sexually transmitted infections** (STIs).

<!-- STEP -->
## Names to know

STIs include:

- gonorrhoea
- herpes
- syphilis
- genital warts
- HIV, which can eventually lead to AIDS

Some of these are not curable yet.

<!-- STEP -->
## Protection and care

Using condoms greatly reduces the risk of passing on these infections, and it also helps prevent pregnancy. No method is perfect.

An infection is a health matter and not a reason for shame. A person who is worried should see a doctor early.
