# What does being ready really mean?

<!-- STEP -->
## Thoughtful decisions

Being ready for sexual activity is not only about physical changes. It is also about being able to make thoughtful and responsible decisions.

Such choices help prevent unplanned pregnancies and sexually transmitted infections, and they support healthy relationships.

<!-- STEP -->
## Pressure and trusted adults

Nobody should be pressured into sexual activity, by a friend or by anyone else, and saying no is always allowed.

If something worries you or you have a question, ask a parent, a doctor, an ASHA worker or another adult you trust.
