# How can unwanted pregnancy be prevented?

<!-- STEP -->
## Barriers

Methods that prevent pregnancy are called **contraceptive** methods. Some of them act as barriers: condoms and vaginal covers stop sperm from reaching the egg.

<!-- STEP -->
## Oral pills

Oral pills are medicines that change the release of eggs by altering hormones, though they may have some side effects.

<!-- STEP -->
## Devices in the uterus

An **Intra-Uterine Device** (IUD), such as the Copper-T, is placed in the uterus to avoid pregnancy. It may sometimes irritate the uterus.

<!-- STEP -->
## Surgical methods

Surgery can block the vas deferens in males or the fallopian tubes in females, so that the sperm and the egg cannot meet.

<!-- STEP -->
## A pill from Lucknow

Indian scientists at the Central Drug Research Institute, Lucknow, developed an oral contraceptive pill. Your book calls it the world's first non-steroidal, non-hormonal oral contraceptive pill. It is provided free through the National Family Planning Programme.

A doctor or an ASHA worker can advise an adult couple on these methods.
