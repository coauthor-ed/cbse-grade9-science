# What determines a baby's biological sex?

<!-- STEP -->
## Two sex chromosomes

Every person has two sex chromosomes. Females have **XX** and males have **XY**.

So every egg carries an X, while a sperm carries either an X or a Y. From this, who do you think determines the sex of a baby?

<!-- STEP -->
## It depends on the sperm

Press **Sperm with X** and **Sperm with Y**. The mother always gives an X, and the father gives either an X or a Y, so the sex of the baby depends on which kind of sperm fuses with the egg.

<!-- STEP -->
## Chance, and nobody's fault

Which sperm reaches the egg is a matter of chance, and nobody chooses it.

Some families blame a mother for having daughters, and that is wrong both in science and in fairness. A mother does not decide the sex of her baby.

**A girl and a boy are equally welcome, and the sex of a baby is never anyone's fault.**
