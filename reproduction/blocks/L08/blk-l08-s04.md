# How does a baby grow during pregnancy?

<!-- STEP -->
## First trimester

Pregnancy in humans lasts about nine months, divided into three stages called **trimesters**.

In the first trimester, the fertilised egg develops into an **embryo** during the first two months, and major organs start forming. From about the ninth week, the embryo is called a **foetus**.

<!-- STEP -->
## Second trimester

The foetus grows bigger and stronger, and the mother can usually feel its movements.

<!-- STEP -->
## Third trimester

The baby grows rapidly and gets ready for life outside the womb.

The uterus protects and nourishes the baby throughout this time. Food and oxygen reach the baby from the mother through the placenta and the cord.

<!-- STEP -->
## Childbirth

During childbirth, strong contractions of the muscles of the uterus help push the foetus out through the birth canal.

If a normal vaginal birth is not possible or not safe for the mother or the foetus, doctors may use medical or surgical procedures to help deliver the baby safely.
