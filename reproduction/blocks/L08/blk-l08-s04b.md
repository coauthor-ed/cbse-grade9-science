# How do we care for a mother and her baby?

<!-- STEP -->
## During pregnancy

A woman's health plays a vital role in her baby's growth and safety. She should eat a balanced diet rich in proteins, vitamins and minerals, attend regular medical check-ups, and follow her doctor's advice on light exercise and rest.

Support from her family helps her remain healthy and free of stress.

<!-- STEP -->
## After the birth

A baby needs special care. Breastfeeding is essential, because a mother's milk provides complete nutrition and protects the infant from many diseases. Newborns must be kept warm, vaccinated on time and handled gently.

The mother needs nutritious food and adequate rest. She should avoid smoking and alcohol, and take medicines only on medical advice.

<!-- STEP -->
## If a mother feels low

Some mothers feel anxious and very tired after a baby is born. This is called **post-partum depression**. It is a recognised condition that can be treated, and it is not the mother's fault.

She should be encouraged to talk to a doctor, a nurse or an ASHA worker, so that she gets proper help and support.

<!-- STEP -->
## ASHA workers

More than 10 lakh Accredited Social Health Activist (ASHA) workers serve across India. These women are trained to promote hygiene, immunisation and family planning, especially in rural areas.

They offer advice on maternal care, safe deliveries and contraceptive methods.
