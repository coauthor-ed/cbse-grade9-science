# How do we care for ourselves during a period?

<!-- STEP -->
## Period is your pride

Menstruation is a sign of a healthy reproductive system. It is not something to be ashamed of, and a girl need not hide it or feel embarrassed to ask for help.

Boys should know these facts too, so that they can be respectful and supportive.

<!-- STEP -->
## Clean products and clean hands

- Use menstrual products, such as sanitary pads (preferably biodegradable ones), tampons or menstrual cups.
- Clean your genital area with water. Avoid soap there, as it can disrupt the natural balance of bacteria.
- Always wash your hands with soap and water, before and after changing your pad.

<!-- STEP -->
## Change and dispose with care

- Wrap used pads in newspaper or their wrapper, and put them in a bin. Do not flush them down the toilet.
- Clean reusable pads as the maker's instructions say, and make sure they are completely dry before the next use.
- Change pads in 4 to 6 hours, or more often if your flow is heavy.

**These habits help you stay healthy and keep your surroundings clean.**
