# Holding the Line for 28 Days

<!-- STEP -->
## A track, and a task

A 28-day track runs across the panel. Every day of it is a decision point, and you are given one task to hold off for the whole cycle: keep a pregnancy from starting, or keep an infection from being passed on, or both.

Somewhere on each day you choose one of the four methods from the board you just worked through.

<!-- STEP -->
## The bars tell you what each one does

Each method comes with a bar showing the step it actually blocks. A barrier stops sperm reaching the egg. A pill changes when the egg is released. A device sits in the uterus. Surgery removes the meeting place.

**A bar that stays empty is not a small effect. It is a gap.**

<!-- STEP -->
## Some choices fail on a given day

A method that stops sperm never touches an infection. A pill that stops ovulation does nothing at all about what is passed between two people.

Choose wrong on a day and the panel shows you plainly which step was left open - not a score, but the reason.

<!-- STEP -->
## Winning

Hold the task off for every day of the cycle and you win. Each method works by stopping **one** specific step, and the step it stops is not the step another method stops.

**Get to day 28 with the task still unmet.**
