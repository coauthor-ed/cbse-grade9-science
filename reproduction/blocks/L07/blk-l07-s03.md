# What are the parts of the male system?

<!-- STEP -->
## In a pouch, outside the abdomen

The testes sit in a pouch of skin called the **scrotum**. If you chose a place inside the abdomen, that was a fair guess, because most organs are safer there.

But sperm need a slightly cooler place than the inside of the body, and the scrotum keeps the testes slightly cooler.

**The position of the testes helps sperm to form.**

<!-- STEP -->
## Two jobs of the testes

The testes make sperm. They also make a **hormone**, which is a chemical that regulates different functions in the body.

This hormone controls sperm production, and it causes the physical changes seen in boys during puberty.

<!-- STEP -->
## The way out

From the testes, sperm travel through a long tube called the **vas deferens**. It opens into the **urethra**, which runs through the penis.

The urethra is a common passage for urine and for sperm.

<!-- STEP -->
## Fluid along the way

Glands add fluid to the sperm along the way. These are the **seminal vesicles** and the **prostate gland**.

The fluid nourishes the sperm, and it helps them stay active and move.

<!-- STEP -->
## Head and tail

Each sperm has a head, which contains its genetic material, and a long tail, which helps it swim towards the egg.
