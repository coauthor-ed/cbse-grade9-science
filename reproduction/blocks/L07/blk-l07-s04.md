# What are the parts of the female system?

<!-- STEP -->
## A pair of ovaries

The **ovaries** are two small organs, one on each side. They produce the eggs, and they also release hormones that bring about the changes of puberty.

So ovaries and testes each do two jobs: they make gametes, and they make hormones.

<!-- STEP -->
## An oviduct from each ovary

An **oviduct**, also called a fallopian tube, connects each ovary to the uterus.

An egg cannot swim. Fine fringes at the end of the tube gather it in, and tiny hairs inside the tube move it along.

<!-- STEP -->
## The uterus

The **uterus** is a bag-like structure with a thick wall, and a foetus develops inside it.

The uterus also has a soft inner lining, which becomes important very soon.

<!-- STEP -->
## The cervix and the vagina

The uterus opens into the **vagina** through a narrow passage called the **cervix**.

<!-- STEP -->
## Where the two routes meet

During sexual intercourse, millions of sperm enter through the vagina. They swim through the uterus and may reach an egg in an oviduct.

The egg's route and the sperm's route meet in that tube. It is not a contest won by the fastest swimmer, and the egg is not waiting still, because the tube keeps moving it along.

**A sperm and an egg meet in an oviduct, not in the uterus.**
