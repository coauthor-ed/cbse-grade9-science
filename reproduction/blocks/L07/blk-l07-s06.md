# One Sperm, One Wrong Tube

Everything the last two slides said has a consequence, and the consequence is a route.

The sperm is released into the vagina, where it has no way to know which way is in. In front of it are the cervix, then the uterus, then two oviducts branching away from the top of the uterus - and only one of those two tubes has an egg in it. The other one is a dead end that runs out of nowhere.

Nobody points the sperm. It beats its tail, it goes up through the cervix, it crosses the uterus, and it picks a tube.

**Get one sperm to the egg. There are millions released, and the counter on the right is counting the ones that actually arrive.**
