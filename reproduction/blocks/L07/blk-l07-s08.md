# What happens when a sperm meets an egg?

<!-- STEP -->
## Egg plus sperm makes one cell

If a sperm meets an egg in the oviduct and succeeds in fusing with it, a **zygote** is formed.

The egg brings 23 chromosomes and the sperm brings 23.

<!-- STEP -->
## Two nuclei become one

The nucleus of the sperm and the nucleus of the egg join, so the zygote has 46 chromosomes in 23 pairs.

One chromosome of each pair comes from each parent. The chromosomes stay whole: they sit side by side and are not blended.

<!-- STEP -->
## Dividing on the way

The zygote now travels along the oviduct towards the uterus. As it travels it divides again and again by mitosis, so one cell becomes two, then four, then eight, and every cell keeps all 46 chromosomes.

<!-- STEP -->
## Into the lining

By now the inner lining of the uterus has become thick and rich in blood vessels. The ball of cells implants into this lining and receives nourishment from it.

**Implantation marks the beginning of pregnancy.**

This means a zygote does not attach as soon as it forms, and the lining is not always ready. What happens to the lining in a month when no egg is fertilised? The next lesson begins there.
