# How does new life begin in human beings?

<!-- STEP -->
## Growing up changes the body

As a child grows into an adult, the reproductive organs mature and begin to make gametes: **sperm** in males and **eggs** in females.

Human beings reproduce sexually, so a new life needs both of these cells. When a sperm meets an egg inside the female body, they form a zygote.

<!-- STEP -->
## Three questions for this lesson

The zygote grows into an embryo and then into a foetus in the uterus. We will take this story in order:

- Where are sperm and eggs made?
- How does each cell form?
- How do the two cells meet?

We start with sperm, which form in two oval organs called **testes** (one is a testis).
