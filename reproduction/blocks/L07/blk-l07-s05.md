# How are sperm and eggs made?

<!-- STEP -->
## Half the chromosomes

Making gametes is called **gametogenesis**, and it takes place in the testes and the ovaries.

You have met the division behind it, meiosis. Human body cells have 46 chromosomes, but sperm and eggs have only 23, so a zygote again has 46.

<!-- STEP -->
## Two very different cells

The testes produce numerous tiny sperm that move actively. The ovaries produce a single large egg at a time.

- Size: a sperm is very small, an egg is large.
- Number made: millions of sperm, few eggs.
- Stored nutrients: absent in a sperm, present in an egg.
- Movement: a sperm swims, an egg cannot.

This difference between male and female gametes is seen across most animals.

<!-- STEP -->
## Seen at one scale

Pictures often draw the two cells at similar sizes, and that is misleading. A human egg can just be seen without a microscope, but a sperm head is far smaller.

The figure now keeps both cells at roughly one scale, so the sperm looks tiny next to the egg.

<!-- STEP -->
## Eggs are not made fresh each month

Many people think a girl makes new eggs every month. In fact, at birth her ovaries already have millions of immature eggs.

From puberty onwards, usually one mature egg is released every month from one of the ovaries. This release is called **ovulation**.
