# Eggs in Open Water

<!-- STEP -->
## A pond

Most fish and frog eggs and young ones are lost, and a pond shows why. Reeds grow at one edge here, and a current moves across the water.

<!-- STEP -->
## Eggs, then sperm

The female fish releases her eggs into the water, and the male releases sperm over them.

<!-- STEP -->
## Outside the body

The gametes meet in the water, outside both bodies. This is called **external fertilisation**, and it happens in most fish and in frogs.

<!-- STEP -->
## What happens to the eggs

A large number of eggs are laid, but many are destroyed by water currents or eaten by other animals.

<!-- STEP -->
## A few are left

Only a few of the eggs come through. **So many eggs laid does not mean many young ones grow up.**
