# One Season, One Budget

<!-- STEP -->
## Three places, one budget

A pond, a patch of warm rock, and a nest on a cliff ledge. Each will hatch young ones this season.

All three come out of the same total store of energy. Every egg laid costs some of it, and how much each egg gets decides what it can become on its own.

You set the number of eggs each site lays and how rich each one is - thousands of lean eggs, or a handful of rich ones.

<!-- STEP -->
## The arithmetic on the panel

The pond does the arithmetic honestly. Yolk spent per egg, eggs at risk in open water, eggs hatched, and what each hatchling can do on its own without being fed.

It will not tell you which site is right. It tells you what each site actually costs.

<!-- STEP -->
## The season ends in winter

At the end of the season, the sites differ in what they leave behind.

A site that laid thousands of lean eggs and lost most of them has kept almost nothing. A site that laid eight rich eggs and guarded all eight has kept a small number - and each of those is worth something on its own.

**Survive the season with a population left behind. Neither more eggs nor fewer eggs wins by itself.**
