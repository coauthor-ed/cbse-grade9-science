# Young Ones and Their Care

<!-- STEP -->
## Inside the mother

A calf is a mammal's young one. Before birth, it develops inside the body of the cow.

<!-- STEP -->
## Milk after birth

Mammals typically feed their young on breast milk for some time after birth. So the mother goes on feeding her young one after it is born.

<!-- STEP -->
## Ready or not

In some species, young ones can find their own food as soon as they are born. In others, they need feeding and care for a long time. This depends broadly on how long the embryo develops inside an egg or inside the mother's body.

<!-- STEP -->
## Two challenges for all animals

All animals face two basic challenges: the male and female gametes must meet, and the young ones must survive long enough to grow and reproduce. Why might most complex animals and flowering plants use sexual reproduction? Variation helps some individuals in changing conditions, while simple organisms can copy quickly when conditions are good.
