## Stems with no roots

A gardener cuts pieces of stem from one good rose bush and pushes each piece into a tray of damp soil. Each **cutting** has a few leaves at the top, but it has no roots and no seed.

<!-- STEP -->
## New leaves open

After some time, new leaves open on every stem. Each cutting is still growing, even though it was cut from the bush.

<!-- STEP -->
## Roots from the stem itself

Look under the soil. Roots have grown from the buried part of each stem, so nothing had to join the cutting from outside.

**One bush has become a tray of young plants, and no seed was used.**
