## Why seed-grown plants differ

Vegetatively propagated plants tend to be more vulnerable to disease than plants grown from seed. Plants from seed differ from each other, so a disease that harms some may leave others alive.

<!-- STEP -->
## When a farmer chooses copies

A farmer chooses asexual methods to grow a large number of plants of one good variety, quickly. Copying is not always better, because it gives sameness and no differences.

**Copying gives speed and sameness, so a whole crop can share one weakness.**
