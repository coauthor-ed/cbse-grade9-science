# The Nursery Orders

<!-- STEP -->
## Three things on the shelf

A nursery has a crate of cuttings, a grafting bench, and a bottle of tissue-cultured plantlets. Three orders come in.

Each order names something the farmer needs, and your job is to fill it the way a gardener would - and to be able to say why the other two do not fit it.

<!-- STEP -->
## The three orders

Order one: five hundred plants of one variety, all the same, needed this season.

Order two: a plant with a wild root system below and a pink flower above it.

Order three: a plantation that must not carry the mosaic virus the present plants have.

<!-- STEP -->
## One answer each

Each order has exactly one answer, and filling one correctly does not help you fill the next. A cutting fills the first. Only a graft gives one plant two different parents. Only a shoot tip grown in a sterile bottle comes out clean of the virus.

**Pick the right thing for each order, and be ready to say why the other two were wrong.**
