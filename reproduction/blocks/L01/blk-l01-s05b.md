## Bury the middle of a twig

Select a flexible, thin twig of a shrub or tree, such as a lemon. Bend it down and bury its middle part under the soil. The twig stays joined to the parent plant.

<!-- STEP -->
## Wait for roots

Water it regularly. After 10-15 days, roots develop from the buried part. Move the slider to count the days.

<!-- STEP -->
## Cut it free

Once roots have developed, cut the twig from the parent plant so that it can grow as a new plant. Press **Cut the twig**.

A cutting starts life on its own, but a layered twig is cut only after its roots have formed.
