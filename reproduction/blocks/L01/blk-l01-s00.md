## A tree dies, its seeds go on

Every living thing has a definite life span: it is born, grows, matures, reproduces and at last dies. A mango tree may grow old and die, but its seeds go on growing as new mango plants.

<!-- STEP -->
## New individuals of their own kind

Cows give birth to calves, dogs to puppies and humans to children.

**Reproduction is the process by which living things produce new individuals of their own kind.**

<!-- STEP -->
## Two main ways

Living things reproduce in two main ways. In **asexual** reproduction, a single parent produces young that are almost exact copies of itself. In **sexual** reproduction, the young get a mix of characters from two individuals, so they differ a little from their parents.

Over many generations, such small differences help living things adapt to changing surroundings.

## Two questions to keep in mind

These lessons will answer two questions:

- When does a farmer prefer asexual methods, and when sexual ones?
- Why do most complex animals and flowering plants use sexual reproduction, while simple organisms like yeast and hydra mainly reproduce asexually?

You may think copying is only for tiny living things, but a rose bush can do it too.
