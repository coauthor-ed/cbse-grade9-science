## Pick the shoots

Watch a gardener at work. Overgrown branches are often cut at the end of the plant's growing season. Shoots for planting are collected in the morning.

<!-- STEP -->
## Count the nodes

On a shoot, a **node** is the point where a leaf grows, and an **internode** is the stretch between two nodes. Count them on this stem.

<!-- STEP -->
## Take off the lower leaves

Remove the leaves from the lower half of the cutting, and leave the upper ones on. Leaves under the soil would only rot. Press **Remove lower leaves**.

<!-- STEP -->
## Plant it at a slant

Push the cutting into soil mixed with compost, up to about half its length, at about 45-60° from the soil surface. Move the slider to set the angle.

<!-- STEP -->
## Water it and watch

Water the cutting regularly and watch for change. Roots form on the buried part, and new leaves open above.

Ask a gardener or farmer near you to show you cutting, grafting and layering. Record what you see in your notebook.
