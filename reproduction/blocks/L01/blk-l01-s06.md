## One shoot tip

The very tip of a shoot is a growing point called the **apical meristem**. A small piece of it is placed on a nutrient gel in a clean bottle.

<!-- STEP -->
## Many plantlets

In the bottle, the tip multiplies into many tiny plantlets. Every one of them comes from one plant, so this too is asexual reproduction.

<!-- STEP -->
## Out to the field

Tissue culture has changed banana farming. Farmers now get mass-produced healthy plantlets, which help eliminate virus-infected plants and ensure high yields.

A banana plant looks like a tree, but it is a giant herb, and farmers usually raise it from side shoots and not from seed.
