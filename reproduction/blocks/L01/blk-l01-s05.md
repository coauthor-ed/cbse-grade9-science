## Two plants

Take a healthy rooted plant (Plant A), such as a wild rose. Take a healthy stem piece from another variety (Plant B), such as a yellow rose. The rooted plant is the **stock** and the stem piece is the **scion**.

<!-- STEP -->
## Slit and fit

Make a slit on a twig of Plant A. Then insert the stem piece of Plant B and fit it into the slit.

<!-- STEP -->
## Wrap it, then cut

Wrap the join with cotton cloth or film to keep pests out until it heals. Then cut the other branches of Plant A, water the plant regularly and watch Plant B grow.

<!-- STEP -->
## Not a mix

A graft does not blend the two plants. The roots stay Plant A's, and the flowers above the join are Plant B's yellow.
