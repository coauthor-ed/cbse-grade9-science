## The new plant matches its parent

The flowers on the new plant are the same deep red as the parent's. It is reasonable to expect a weaker plant, since a cutting starts with no roots. But a cutting is a piece of the parent, so it carries what the parent carries, and it grows its own roots from the stem.

<!-- STEP -->
## Many plants grow this way

New plants that arise from the growing parts of a plant show **vegetative propagation**. Tap a name to see the part that grows:

- Potato and ginger sprout from fleshy underground stems.
- Money plant and sugarcane grow from stem cuttings.
- *Bryophyllum* sprouts tiny plantlets along its leaves.

## One parent

**Vegetative propagation involves only one parent, so the new plants are genetically identical to it.**
