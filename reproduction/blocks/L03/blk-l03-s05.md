## Each pair doubles the count

Every new pair gives two choices, so the count doubles each time. Move the slider to add pairs.

## Twenty-three pairs

From the sorting of pairs alone, a person with 23 pairs can make 2 multiplied by itself 23 times: 83,88,608 kinds of gamete. That is over 83 lakh.

<!-- STEP -->
## Two parents

About 800 crore people are alive, so 83 lakh alone is not enough to make everyone different. But a child comes from one gamete of each parent: 83 lakh x 83 lakh, about 70 lakh crore possible children. That is about 8,800 times the number of people alive.

So brothers and sisters differ. Identical twins are the exception, since they come from one zygote.
