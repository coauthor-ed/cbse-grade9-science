## Two different cells

In humans the sperm can swim, and the egg is much larger and does not swim. Each carries 23 chromosomes. This figure is not to scale.

<!-- STEP -->
## Neither grows into a person alone

A human sperm or egg cannot grow into a new person by itself. The two must meet and join.

<!-- STEP -->
## The full set comes back

When they join, 23 + 23 = 46 chromosomes. The full set is back, and a new individual begins.
