# The Breeding Bench

A bench with a pool of parents on one side and a target population card on the other.

Pick two parents, cross them, and the bench breeds a generation. Each gamete takes one version of each character, so every offspring in that generation gets one version of each character too - but which one is down to chance.

Cross the same pair again and you get more of the same. Cross two different pairs and the combinations spread out.

Your two targets are on the card: **every individual still carries the wanted character**, and **as many different combinations as possible**.

You have as many rounds as you need. Nothing is counting down.
