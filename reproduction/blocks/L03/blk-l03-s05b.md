## Everyone is a new mix

This random mixing makes children genetically different from their parents and from each other. Each of us receives a unique combination of chromosomes, and this creates **variation**.

<!-- STEP -->
## Two examples

Some people can tolerate low oxygen at high altitudes, and some can digest milk as adults.

Variation helps some individuals adapt better to changing surroundings. Over time, this contributes to evolution.
