## Beads stand for characters

Take three pairs of beads: green, blue and red. Each pair stands for one pair of chromosomes, and its two beads stand for two versions of a character.

<!-- STEP -->
## One from each pair

A gamete gets one chromosome from each pair, so pick one bead from each pair without looking. Press **Draw**. Light green, light blue and light red means blonde and straight hair with brown eyes.

<!-- STEP -->
## Eight kinds

Keep drawing. Each draw gives either the same set or a new one, and three pairs give eight kinds in all.

The beads show only how pairs sort. Real hair and eye colour depend on many parts of the chromosomes, so a child can have an in-between shade.
