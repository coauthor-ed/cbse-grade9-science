# Do All Flowers Have Every Part?

<!-- STEP -->
## Keep a record

Collect a few flowers near you. Look at each one from the outer ring to the inner, and write yes or no for each part. Flower A in this table has no stamens, so it makes no pollen.

<!-- STEP -->
## Other flowers differ

Flowers B, C and D are made-up examples of what you may find. B has all four parts, which makes it a **complete flower**. C has stamens but no pistil, so it can give pollen but cannot form a fruit.

<!-- STEP -->
## Other features

The table has a column for other features. In some flowers both the sepals and the petals are **fused**, which means they are joined together. Flower D is like this.

<!-- STEP -->
## Guess the job of each part

The last column asks you to guess each part's job from what you can see.

- **Sepal:** protects the bud
- **Petal:** attracts visitors with colour and scent
- **Stamen:** makes pollen and holds it up
- **Pistil:** receives pollen and holds the ovules
