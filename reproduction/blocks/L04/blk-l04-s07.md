# How Far Must Pollen Travel?

<!-- STEP -->
## A very short trip

In a pea flower, pollen moves only a tiny distance. The anthers sit right beside the stigma inside the closed flower, so no outside help is needed.

<!-- STEP -->
## A longer trip

Now think of flower A and flower C in the table. Flower A has a pistil and no stamens, and flower C has stamens and no pistil. For A to form a fruit, pollen must leave one flower and reach another, and neither flower can move.

**Pollen grains have no legs and no wings. What carries them from flower to flower?**
