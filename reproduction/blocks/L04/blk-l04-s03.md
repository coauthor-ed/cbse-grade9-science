# Four Pods from Five Flowers

<!-- STEP -->
## One flower gave no pod

Only the young bud with its stamens removed gave no pod. The other four all did, including three flowers inside bags, so **a muslin bag does not stop every pod**.

<!-- STEP -->
## Pea flowers pollinate early

A pea flower's stamens shed pollen onto its own stigma before the flower opens. A bag keeps pollen from outside away, but it cannot stop the flower's own pollen inside it.

<!-- STEP -->
## Why the two cut flowers differ

A young, less developed bud had not yet shed any pollen, so removing its stamens took away its only pollen. The open flower already had pollen on its stigma before the stamens were removed.

<!-- STEP -->
## What we can infer

We infer that pollen reached the stigma of every flower that gave a pod, and that none reached the one flower that gave no pod.

**The transfer of pollen grains from the anther to the stigma of a flower is called pollination. It is necessary for a fruit to form.**
