# Why an Unused Preparation Is Not a Mistake

## The objection, taken seriously

The lining thickens, and no zygote arrives to use it. So the body did a piece of work for nothing.

That reasoning is sound as far as it goes, and it is worth taking seriously rather than waving away - a system that built the same expensive preparation on every single cycle and never adjusted would be wasteful.

## What the lining actually is

Read the lining's job off the fertilisation slide. It thickens, and it grows blood vessels, and it does both of those things **before** a zygote reaches it. Those blood vessels are there to feed something that has arrived but has not yet grown a way to feed itself.

So the lining is not a general-purpose preparation. It is a specific one, built for a particular arrival, and it is built in advance of it.

## Why building in advance wins

Ask what the alternative would be.

- **Wait to start.** If the body began thickening its lining only once a zygote was already there, the zygote would arrive at an unprepared wall - and it implants, it does not wait. The preparation would have to be finished before the thing that needs it turns up, which means starting before it arrives anyway.
- **Build in advance.** The lining is ready on the day the zygote turns up, and on the days it does not, the preparation is simply not used.

**A body that can become pregnant keeps itself ready. An unused preparation is the price of that readiness, not a sign that it failed.**

## The pattern behind it

This is the same trade the chapter keeps making. The testes hang outside the body all the time to keep one number right, whether or not sperm are being made at that moment. The egg waits in the oviduct whether or not a sperm comes. Each of those looks like effort spent in advance, and each of them is the thing that makes the meeting possible at all.
