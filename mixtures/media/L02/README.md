# Media provenance - L02

Per `docs/copyright-and-sourcing.md`: never extract the textbook's own photographs or illustrations; source an openly-licensed equivalent and record its source and licence here.

## Why there is no portrait of Dr Dilip Mahalanabis

- Commons was searched for `Dilip Mahalanabis`, `Mahalanabis`, `oral rehydration`, `ORS packet health` and `oral rehydration solution ORS scientist`. No photograph of him exists there.
- The one portrait on English Wikipedia (`File:Dilip Mahalanabis.jpg`) is `Fair use`, artist and publication unknown. It may not be reused or mirrored.
- A generated portrait of a real person is not acceptable at any licence. The textbook's own illustration may not be extracted.

The slide teaches the proportion, so a scene does the work instead: 100 dots for the camp numbers and a jug with one packet. If a portrait with a clear free licence turns up later, add it here with a licence row.
