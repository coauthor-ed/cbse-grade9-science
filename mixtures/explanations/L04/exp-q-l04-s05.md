# Why a Tall Tower Separates Close Liquids

<!-- STEP -->
## One flask has one temperature

A flask gives one mixed vapour. If two liquids boil only a few degrees apart, that vapour holds a lot of both, and cooling it gives a mixture again.

A tower is different, because it has a different temperature at each height.

<!-- STEP -->
## Each level is cooler than the one below

As the vapour rises, it reaches levels that are cooler and cooler. At each level, the part that cannot stay a vapour at that temperature turns to liquid and is drawn off. The rest keeps rising.

**The fractions leave in order of boiling point: lowest at the top, highest at the bottom.**
