# Solubility Is Not a Percentage

<!-- STEP -->
## Two different bottoms

Both numbers use the same 36 g of salt, but they compare it with different things.

| Number | Compares the salt with | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Solubility | 100 g of **water** | 36 g per 100 g of water |
| % m/m | the whole **solution**, 136 g | (36 / 136) × 100 = 26.5 % |

<!-- STEP -->
## Why they are different

Solubility asks how much the water can dissolve, so it counts the water alone. Concentration asks what part of the mixture is solute, so it counts everything.

**Check the bottom of the fraction: water for solubility, solution for concentration.**
