# Where Should the Water Stand?

Take a strip of paper 3 cm wide and draw a straight pencil line across it, 2 cm from the bottom. Put a black sketch-pen spot in the middle of that line.

The strip now goes into a gas jar with a thin layer of water at the bottom, so that its lower end dips into the water.

**Predict:** Where should the water level be at the start, and why?
