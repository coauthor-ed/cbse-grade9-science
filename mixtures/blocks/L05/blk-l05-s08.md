# What the Strip Tells You

<!-- STEP -->
## Count the spots

Lift the strip out and let it dry. The colours stay where they stopped, so you can count them. Three spots tell you the ink holds at least three dyes.

The words "at least" matter. Two dyes that travel at the same speed stay together and look like one spot, so the count can be too low.

<!-- STEP -->
## A method for tiny amounts

The whole experiment starts from one small spot of ink, so each colour on the strip is a tiny amount. You could cut out one colour and soak it in water, but you would get only a trace of dye.

Compare this with the last two lessons. Crystallization gave you a dish of crystals, and distillation gave you a flask of liquid.

**Chromatography is the method for finding out what is in a mixture, even when you have only a drop of it.**
