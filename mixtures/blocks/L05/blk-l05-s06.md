# Challenge: Make the Colour Spots

<!-- STEP -->
## What each round asks

Each round gives you one sample and a card. The card says how many colour spots the strip must show at the end. To win the round you need all three of these:

- the **spot above the solvent** at the start
- a **solvent that dissolves** that sample
- the **number of spots** that the card asks for

<!-- STEP -->
## What you can change

- The **pencil line**: move it up or down the strip.
- The **solvent level**: raise it or lower it in the jar.
- The **solvent bottle**: choose one of the four.

Each card starts with one thing set up wrong. Run it once to see what goes wrong, then fix it.

<!-- STEP -->
## Two ways a run goes wrong

If the solvent level is above the pencil line, the spot dissolves into the jar and the strip stays bare.

If the pencil line is too high, the solvent has too little paper left to climb. The colours run out of room before they can separate, so they stay as one smear.
