# Crystals You Have Seen

<!-- STEP id="rock-salt" -->
## Rock salt

These are crystals of rock salt, each about one to two centimetres across. Their flat faces and square corners come from the regular pattern of particles inside.

The salt in your kitchen is also made of crystals, only much smaller.

<!-- STEP id="mishri" -->
## Mishri, or candy sugar

Mishri is made from a saturated sugar solution. The solution is left to stand, and sugar crystals grow in it until they are large enough to lift out.

This is crystallization at home. Nothing happens to the sugar except that it comes out of solution slowly.

<!-- STEP id="frost" -->
## Frost and snowflakes

Frost on a window is made of crystals too, and so are snowflakes, but neither comes from a solution. Water vapour in the air turns directly into ice crystals.

**Rock salt, mishri and frost are all crystals: solids with their particles in a regular pattern.**
