## Mawsmai Cave

This is Mawsmai Cave at Sohra (Cherrapunji) in Meghalaya. Water seeps through the rock with minerals dissolved in it, and those minerals come out as solid, slowly, layer on layer.

Over a very long time, this has coated the walls and ceiling with pale formations.

## Mines, caves and the Earth's crust

Large crystal deposits are found in mines, in caves and inside the Earth's crust. Quartz is one of the beautiful crystals found in nature.

**A solid can come out of water in a beaker in an hour, or in a cave over thousands of years.**
