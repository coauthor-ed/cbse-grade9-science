# From 70 °C Down to 20 °C

A saturated solution of compound B was made at **70 °C** by dissolving **320 g** of it in **100 g** of water.

The solubility curve for 100 g of water gives two readings: **320 g** at 70 °C and **205 g** at 20 °C.

The solution now cools slowly to 20 °C. Nothing is added and nothing is taken out.

**Predict:** How much solid comes out of the solution?
