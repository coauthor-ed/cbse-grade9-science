# What If You Want the Liquid Back?

## What you have now

Dry blue crystals sit on the watch glass. The liquid left in the beaker is not pure water, because it is still a saturated solution with some copper sulfate in it.

Crystallization gave you the solid, and so did the salt pan. In a salt pan, the water went off into the air and was lost.

<!-- STEP -->
## Now the liquid is the part you need

Sometimes the liquid is what you need. Salt water has salt in it, but you may need the water. Evaporation gets you the salt and loses the water.

Here is a harder case. A student in a laboratory mixes acetone and water by accident. Both are liquids, and they mix completely, so liquids like these are called **miscible**. Leave the mixture out and both slowly evaporate into the air.
