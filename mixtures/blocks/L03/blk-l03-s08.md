# Challenge: Make the Crystals

<!-- STEP -->
## What you must make

Each round gives you a hot saturated solution and names the mass of crystals you must make.

When you stop, the crystals must be:

- **pure**
- **dry**
- the **right mass**

<!-- STEP -->
## What you decide

- **How far to cool.** Read the solubility curve and work out the temperature that gives that mass.
- **The order of the steps:** filter hot, cool, filter off the crystals, rinse and dry. If you cool before the hot filter, sand ends up with your crystals.

<!-- STEP -->
## Look out for round 2

The solubility of common salt hardly changes with temperature. Cool it all the way from 80 °C to 10 °C, and only about 1 g comes out.

Read the curve before you expect more.
