# Challenge: Test It, Then Name It

<!-- STEP -->
## A jar with no label

Each round gives you one jar with nothing written on it, and you may not taste it.

First say what you think from the look alone. Then use the tests from the bench: the **clock**, the **filter paper** and the **laser**.

<!-- STEP -->
## The tests decide

There are two bins: **Homogeneous** and **Heterogeneous**.

- A bright line under the laser means heterogeneous.
- A layer after ten minutes means heterogeneous.
- Something left on the paper means heterogeneous.
- If all three tests find nothing, it is homogeneous.

You can file a jar after you have run at least one test.

**One test can miss something, so run more than one when you are not sure.**

<!-- STEP -->
## How you win

You win a round when the jar is in the right bin. If it is in the wrong bin, you will see which test shows why.

One jar is cloudy like chalk water but behaves differently, so watch it closely. The last jar is a dry powder, and for that one you get a hand lens.

There is no clock and no penalty. A wrong answer just means you try again.
