# Three Beakers, One Bench

<!-- STEP -->
## Three things stirred into water

Three beakers stand on a bench, each with 50 mL of water. Something different has been stirred into each one.

- **A**: one spatula of common salt
- **B**: one spatula of chalk powder
- **C**: a few drops of packet milk

A looks like plain water. B and C are both cloudy, so it is natural to think they are the same kind of mixture.

<!-- STEP -->
## Look closer

Tap a beaker to see it through a hand lens.

In B you can see tiny white grains floating in the water. In C you see no grains at all, only an even cloudiness. A shows nothing but clear liquid.

So your eyes can find the grains in B, but they cannot tell you what is in C.

<!-- STEP -->
## Two things on the bench

A laser pointer stands beside the beakers. You will send its thin beam through each mixture and look from the side, at a right angle to the beam.

**Do not look directly into a laser beam. It can damage your eyes for life.**

There is also a clock, because you will leave the mixtures undisturbed and see what changes.

**B and C both look cloudy. Are they the same kind of mixture?**
