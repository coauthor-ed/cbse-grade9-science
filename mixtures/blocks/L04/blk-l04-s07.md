# Challenge: Distil to Order

<!-- STEP -->
## What you get

You get a distillation set-up and a card with two liquids and their boiling points.

First, label the flask, the condenser and the receiver.

<!-- STEP -->
## What you do

- **Decide first:** will simple distillation separate this pair? The two boiling points tell you.
- **Run it:** heat one minute at a time and watch the graph.
- **Change the receiver** when the reading starts to climb again.

<!-- STEP -->
## Close pairs

Some cards hold two liquids that are close together. On those the graph never goes flat, because both liquids come over in every drop.

**You win a round when each receiver holds what the thermometer said it would.**
