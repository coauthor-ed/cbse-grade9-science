# What Heat Cannot Separate

<!-- STEP -->
## What these methods used

Every separation uses a difference between the parts. Here are the ones you have used so far.

- **Crystallization** uses a difference in solubility.
- **Distillation** uses a difference in boiling point.
- **Fractional distillation** uses boiling point too, with a tall tower in place of a flask.

All of these use heating or cooling.

<!-- STEP -->
## A mixture that heat cannot separate

Now look at black ink from a sketch pen, which is a mixture of dyes in water. You might try to distil it. The water would boil off and collect, and the dyes would stay behind in the flask, still mixed together as a dark stain.

Dyes do not boil apart from one another. Strong heat only burns them, so distillation can never give you each colour on its own.

## A different difference

To separate the dyes, we need a difference that does not depend on boiling. Try this at home: put a drop of water on a line drawn with a black sketch pen.
