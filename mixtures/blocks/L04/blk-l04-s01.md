# Two Liquids in One Flask

A flask holds a mixture of two liquids that mix completely, **liquid A** and **liquid B**. You cannot tell by looking which is which.

Their bottles give the boiling points: **A boils at 60 °C** and **B boils at 90 °C**.

The flask is heated. The vapour is cooled and turns back into liquid, and that liquid drips into a separate vessel.

**Predict:** What comes over into the vessel as the temperature rises?
