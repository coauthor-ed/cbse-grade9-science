# Every Method Uses a Difference

<!-- STEP -->
## What each method used

Look back at the mixtures in this lesson. Tap each one and see what difference between its parts did the work.

| Mixture | Method | Difference used |
|---|---|---|
| Mustard oil and water | Separating funnel | Density |
| Iron nails and sawdust | Magnet | Magnetism |
| Camphor and sand | Heat under a funnel | Only one sublimes |

**Every separation uses a difference between the parts.**

<!-- STEP -->
## When no difference can be reached

Brass is on the bench as well, and it has no entry in the last column. Its metals are mixed evenly all the way through, so a filter, a magnet and a funnel have nothing to work with.

So before you choose a tool, ask what is different about the parts. That question decides the method.

## Next: muddy water

Stir some soil into a tube of water and leave it to stand. The heavier grains settle at the bottom, but the water can stay cloudy for hours. Pour it through filter paper and it often stays cloudy.

The grains are heavier than water, so the difference is there, but it works too slowly. Next we look at ways to make it work faster.
