# Challenge: Four Cards

<!-- STEP -->
## What each round asks

Each round gives you one mixture and a card that says what to do:

- *drain the water out of this funnel and keep the oil*
- *get the camphor back and leave the sand*
- *take the nails out of this sawdust*
- *separate this brass*

<!-- STEP -->
## What you can change

- The **stopcock**: first say which layer leaves first, then open it one splash at a time and close it near the line.
- The **heat** under the china dish, and the **cotton plug** in the funnel.
- The **magnet**: slide it right across the tray.
- **No tool:** one card has no tool that works, and saying so is the right answer.

<!-- STEP -->
## Two ways a round goes wrong

If you open the stopcock one splash too many, oil gets into the water flask.

If you push the heat past the melting point, the camphor melts instead of subliming, and it no longer collects as a clean solid on the funnel.
