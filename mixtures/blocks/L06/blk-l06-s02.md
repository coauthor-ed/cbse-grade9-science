# The Funnel on Its Stand

A **separating funnel** is a glass vessel with a tap at the bottom, called a **stopcock**. Pour 5 mL of mustard oil and 20 mL of water into a 50 mL separating funnel and let it stand.

The liquids form two layers, with the yellow oil on top. A conical flask stands under the funnel to collect what comes out.

**Predict:** You take out the glass stopper at the top and open the stopcock slowly. What comes out first?
