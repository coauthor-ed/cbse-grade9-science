# One Glass, Every Method

## A glass of lemonade

Your textbook asks a simple question about lemonade. A glass of it holds five things: **water**, **sugar**, **citric acid** (the sour part of the lemon), a yellow **colour**, and small bits of **pulp**.

Can you get all five back, each in its own jar? You now know many methods, so here is the test. You may pick one method and run it once.

**Predict:** Which choice gets all five parts back on their own?
