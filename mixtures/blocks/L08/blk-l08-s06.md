# Challenge: Build a Mixture

<!-- STEP -->
## What each round asks

Each round gives you a goal, such as a mixture that scatters the beam and has not settled. The goal describes what the jar must do, and it never gives a name.

<!-- STEP -->
## Your controls

- **Particle size**: five sizes from 0.5 nm to 5000 nm, with marks at 1 nm and 1000 nm.
- **Emulsifying agent**: none, a little or a lot, in the last round only.
- **Shake and wait**: this mixes the jar and then leaves it to stand.

Set your choice before you shake, because the three results appear only after the run.

<!-- STEP -->
## Read the jar

After each run, the jar tells you whether the beam shows, whether anything has separated out, and whether the mixture is the same everywhere. If you miss a goal, change your choice and try again. Nothing is timed.
