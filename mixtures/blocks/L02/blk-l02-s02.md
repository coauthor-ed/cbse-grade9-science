# Add Water and Watch the Number

## A jar of salt solution

A jar holds **10 g of salt dissolved in 90 g of water**. That is 100 g of solution, and 10 of those grams are salt, so the solution is 10 % salt by mass.

You pour in **10 g more water** and stir. No salt has been added or taken out.

**Predict:** What is the percentage of salt by mass now?
