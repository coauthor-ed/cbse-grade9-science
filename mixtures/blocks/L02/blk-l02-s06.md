# Challenge: Make the Jar on the Card

<!-- STEP -->
## What you will do

Each card asks for a solution, such as *250 mL of 4 % m/v saline*. Work out the amounts first, and then make it.

Read the letters after the % sign first, because they tell you what to measure.

- **m/m:** weigh the solute and the water.
- **m/v:** weigh the solid, then fill the flask up to the mark.
- **Dilution** means making a solution weaker by adding water. Measure the **stock solution** (a stronger solution kept ready), then fill the flask up to the mark.

<!-- STEP -->
## The jar prints its own label

Set your amounts and press **Make it**. No percentage shows before you press, so do the working first.

The jar then prints what you really made. If it matches the card, the next card opens. If it does not, you will see what went wrong, and you can change your amounts and try again.

<!-- STEP -->
## The last card

The last card is ORS, with the amounts the World Health Organization gives for one litre of water. Something close will not pass.

There is no clock, and a wrong jar costs you nothing.
