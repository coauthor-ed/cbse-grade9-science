# Is Blood a Suspension?

<!-- STEP -->
## It comes apart like one

Look at the spun tube again. A centrifuge separated the parts of blood, just as it separated mud from water. Blood also coagulates, which is what the alum made the mud do.

So you might say that blood is a suspension. A solution could not be spun apart like this.

<!-- STEP -->
## But you cannot see its cells

The particles of a suspension can be seen with the naked eye, and you cannot see a single cell in blood that way. You need a microscope. Turn up the magnification and compare.

Blood also stays evenly mixed as it flows through your body, the way a solution does, while mud stirred into water keeps sinking. So is blood a solution?

<!-- STEP -->
## Two clues that do not agree

The spin says blood is not a solution. Your eye says blood is not a suspension. Both clues are real, so they cannot simply be ignored.
