# Challenge: One Jar, One Goal

<!-- STEP -->
## Read the goal first

Each round puts one jar in front of you and gives you a goal, such as getting plasma from a tube of blood. The goal decides the method, so read it before you touch anything.

<!-- STEP -->
## Two tools

- **Spin**: the tube goes into the centrifuge, and you set the speed. This helps when the parts differ in density and the sample fits in a tube.
- **Clump**: alum goes into the water, and you set the dose. This helps when the grains are too fine to settle or to be filtered.

If the dose is wrong or the spin is too slow, the jar stays as it was and you can try again.

<!-- STEP -->
## Order matters

The last round has a whole tank of pond water and takes three steps: **settle, then clump, then filter**. Let the heavy grains settle first, so that they do not use up the alum. Then clump the fine grains. Filter last, because the paper can now stop the clumps.
