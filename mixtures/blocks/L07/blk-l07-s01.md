# Spin It and See

Find a partner, cross your arms and hold each other's hands, then spin round together. As you go faster, you feel pulled outwards, away from the middle of the turn. This game is a folk dance called *phugadi* in Marathi and *kikli* in Punjabi.

A **centrifuge** is a machine that spins tubes in a similar way. Here its tubes hold muddy water that stayed cloudy after filtering, and each tube swings out and lies on its side as the machine runs.

**Predict:** Where do the mud particles go inside the spinning tube?
