# The first look at cells

<!-- STEP -->
## Making small things look large

A convex lens makes an object appear larger, and this is called **magnification**. A microscope uses two lenses together: an objective lens and an eyepiece.

<!-- STEP -->
## Hooke and the slice of cork

In 1665, **Robert Hooke** looked at a thin slice of cork through a microscope of his own design. It magnified about 200 to 300 times, written 200-300X.

<!-- STEP -->
## Boxes called cells

Hooke saw small box-like compartments, and he named them "cells". The word means small rooms, like those of monks.

Cork is dead tissue, so these boxes were empty: he was seeing only the walls. Living cells would not be seen properly for some years.
