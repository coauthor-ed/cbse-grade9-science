## Onion Peel

Onion peel cells are box-shaped and arranged in regular rows, like bricks in a wall. A dark nucleus shows in many of them.

<!-- STEP -->
## Cheek Cells

Cheek cells are irregular in shape, and they lie scattered instead of in rows. Press **Onion peel** and **Cheek cells** to compare the two fields.

<!-- STEP -->
## Why the Difference?

Both slides show cells with a boundary and a nucleus, yet only the onion cells keep a straight-sided, regular shape. **Why do you think this difference exists?** A strong sugar solution gives a way to test your idea.
