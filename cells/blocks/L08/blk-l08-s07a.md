## A Plant Slide

Take a thin peel from an onion leaf or from a Rhoeo (cradle lily) leaf and lay it on a glass slide. Add **safranin**, a red stain, and lower a cover slip over it.

Cells are nearly colourless, so a stain gives the contrast you need to see them.

<!-- STEP -->
## A Cheek Slide

Gently scrape the inner side of your cheek with a cotton swab or the blunt end of a toothpick. Spread what you collect on a clean glass slide.

<!-- STEP -->
## Water, Then Blue Stain

Add a drop of water to the cheek cells, then a few drops of **methylene blue** stain, and carefully place a cover slip on top. Now both slides are ready for the microscope.
