# How big is one onion cell?

<!-- STEP -->
## Measure the field

You cannot put a ruler on a cell, so you measure the field of view instead. Place a clear ruler on the stage and read the width of the round field. Here it is 5 mm.

<!-- STEP -->
## Change to micrometres

Cells are measured in micrometres, and 1 mm is 1000 µm. So the field is 5 x 1000 = 5000 µm across.

<!-- STEP -->
## Count the cells

Now replace the ruler with an onion peel slide. Count the cells along one straight line across the field. Here there are 25.

<!-- STEP -->
## Divide

Estimated size of one cell = field width / number of cells = 5000 µm / 25 = 200 µm.

**This is an estimate, because cells differ a little from one another.**
