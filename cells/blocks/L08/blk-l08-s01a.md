# The limit of your eye

## Bring the dots closer

<!-- STEP -->
## About 0.1 mm

From about 25 cm, which is the near point of the human eye, two points about 0.1 mm apart can be seen as separate. If they are closer than this, they look like a single point.

<!-- STEP -->
## The limit of resolution

This gap of 0.1 mm is the **limit of resolution** of the human eye. It tells us the smallest gap at which you can still see two things as two.

**Most cells are smaller than this gap, which is why you cannot see them.**
