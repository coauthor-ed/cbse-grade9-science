# From boxes to living cells

<!-- STEP -->
## Living things in a drop of water

Not long after Hooke, Antonie van Leeuwenhoek made his own small lenses and saw tiny living organisms moving in water. From then on, a cell was something alive, not only an empty box.

## The edge of a cell

With a microscope you can see that every cell has an edge. Whatever enters or leaves a cell must cross this edge, so it matters what the edge lets through. We look at that next.
