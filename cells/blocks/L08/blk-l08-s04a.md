# Inside a school microscope

<!-- STEP -->
## The path of the light

In a school light microscope, the **mirror** sends light up through the slide on the **stage**. The light then passes through the **objective lens** and the **body tube**, and it reaches your eye at the **eyepiece**.

<!-- STEP -->
## Two lenses

The objective lens sits close to the slide, and the eyepiece sits close to your eye. Both of them magnify. Schools usually change between objective lenses such as 10X and 40X.

<!-- STEP -->
## Focusing and holding

The **coarse adjustment knob** makes large movements and the **fine adjustment knob** makes small ones, so that you can bring the slide into focus. Some books print "course", but the word is coarse, which means rough.

You carry the microscope by the **handle**, and the **base** keeps it steady.
