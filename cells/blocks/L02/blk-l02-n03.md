# Is bigger always clearer?

<!-- STEP -->
## Magnification alone

You might expect that more magnification always shows more. Press **More magnification**. The picture grows, but after a point it only gets larger and more blurred, and no new detail appears.

<!-- STEP -->
## Resolution

**Resolution** is a measure of clarity. Press **Better resolution**, and the edges inside the cell become sharp at the same size.

<!-- STEP -->
## Contrast

**Contrast** is the difference in brightness between parts of an object. Press **More contrast**, and the nucleus stands out against the rest of the cell.

**Better microscopes improve all three features: resolution, contrast and magnification.**
