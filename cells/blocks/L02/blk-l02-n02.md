# How much does a microscope magnify?

<!-- STEP -->
## Multiply the two lenses

Total magnification = magnification of the eyepiece x magnification of the objective lens. With 10X and 10X, the total is 100X. A cell 200 µm across then looks 100 times larger: 200 x 100 = 20,000 µm, which is 20 mm.

<!-- STEP -->
## Change the objective

Turn to the 40X objective and the total is 10 x 40 = 400X. The same cell now looks 80 mm across. The cell has not changed, only the image.
