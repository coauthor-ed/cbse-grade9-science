## The Cell Wall

Plants generally cannot move from place to place, so they must withstand wind and rain where they stand. The **cell wall** is a rigid covering outside the cell membrane.

It helps leaves and flowers remain firm and keep their shapes, and it helps a plant stay upright.

<!-- STEP -->
## Rigid but Not Sealed

A rigid wall sounds like a closed box, but it is **permeable**: water and some dissolved minerals can pass through it. That is how water and nutrients from the soil can reach the membrane of a root cell.
