## A Tree in Wind and Rain

A neem tree stands through wind and rain for many years, and it cannot walk away to shelter. Each of its cells is bounded by a membrane that is thin and fluid. That does not sound strong enough to hold a tree up.

## One More Layer

Cells of plants, fungi and bacteria have one more layer around the cell membrane, called the **cell wall**. Animal cells do not have one.

**What do you think a cell wall is needed for?** Keep your guess in mind. You can test it with two slides that you can make in a school laboratory.
