## Two Layers, Different Permeability

It is easy to assume that the outermost layer controls what enters a cell. The wall does not. Compare the two layers:

| | Cell wall | Cell membrane |
|---|---|---|
| Permeability | Permeable: water and some dissolved minerals pass through | Selectively permeable: allows some substances, blocks others |
| Found in | Plants, fungi, bacteria | All cells |
| Nature | Rigid | Thin and fluid |

**The wall gives firmness. The membrane controls what passes.**
