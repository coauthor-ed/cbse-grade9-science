## Far Too Thin to See

The cell membrane is only about **7 to 10 nanometres (nm)** thick. One nanometre is 0.000001 mm, so a membrane 10 nm thick measures 0.00001 mm.

Your eye can tell two points apart only when they are about 0.1 mm away from each other. That gap is 10,000 times wider than the membrane.

## Made of Two Things

In the previous lesson, this boundary let water through while it held back sugar and salt. It does all of that with only two main materials: **lipids** (fats) and **proteins**.

How those molecules are arranged explains how the membrane behaves, so we begin with one lipid molecule.
