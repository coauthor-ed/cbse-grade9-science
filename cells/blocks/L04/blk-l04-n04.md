## Rhoeo Under Sugar

Rhoeo cells have a coloured inner content, which makes this easy to see. Drag **Minutes in sugar** from 0 to 30 and watch one cell.

The outer boundary stays exactly where it was. The coloured content shrinks, and the space between the inner and outer boundaries grows.

<!-- STEP -->
## Two Boundaries Come Apart

The cells lose water by osmosis, so the inner content shrinks and the cell membrane pulls away from the cell wall. This is called **plasmolysis**. The wall does not change in size, because it is rigid.

<!-- STEP -->
## Cheek Cells Under Sugar

Drag **Minutes in sugar** again, this time for cheek cells. A cheek cell also loses water, but it has no wall outside its membrane, so the whole cell shrinks considerably.

<!-- STEP -->
## What the Wall Does

Your guess can now meet the evidence. Both cells lost water, and only the plant cell kept its outline.

**The rigid cell wall helps a plant cell stay firm in its original shape.**
