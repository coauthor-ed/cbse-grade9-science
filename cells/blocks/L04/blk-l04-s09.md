## Hold the Shape

Next you will meet eight cells, each placed in a liquid. Some have a wall and some do not, and the liquid may be plain water, a strong solution, or one that matches the inside of the cell.

For each one, ask two questions: which way does most of the water move, and is there a rigid wall? Then pick what you expect to see. If you pick wrongly, the cell shows what really happens and comes back later.
