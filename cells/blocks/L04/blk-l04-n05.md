## Your Turn: Fixed in One Place

**Why might a wall matter more to a plant, which usually stays in one place, than to an animal, which usually moves?** Think about these points before you answer:

- A plant cannot move away from wind or heavy rain.
- A tall plant has no bones to hold it up.
- An animal needs its tissues to bend, stretch and change shape.

Say your argument aloud in two or three sentences, using the words rigid and flexible.
