## What a Wall Is Made Of

A plant cell wall is made mainly of **cellulose**, a carbohydrate formed from many glucose units linked together. You eat it in fruits and vegetables, where it acts as **roughage** and helps in digestion.

<!-- STEP -->
## Who Has a Wall?

Fungi and bacteria also have a cell wall, which gives protection and structural support. A fungal wall is made of different material from a plant wall.

Animal cells have no wall, so they can change shape easily. This flexibility supports the movement and working of animal tissues.
