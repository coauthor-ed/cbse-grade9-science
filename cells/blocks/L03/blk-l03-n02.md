# Why did the potato change?

<!-- STEP -->
## In plain water

The cells at the surface of the potato hold dissolved substances, and plain water has almost none. So the net movement of water is into the cells, and the piece swells.

<!-- STEP -->
## In salt solution

The solution outside holds more solute than the cells do. So the net movement of water is out of the cells, and the piece shrinks. The salt stays outside the membrane.

<!-- STEP -->
## In plant roots

The same process works in the soil. Water from the soil enters root cells by osmosis.
