# The edge of every cell

<!-- STEP -->
## Where a cell meets the world

Every cell, even a single-celled organism, takes in materials and gives others out. All of this happens at the boundary of the cell, so that is where we look to explain the potato.

<!-- STEP -->
## The cell membrane

The **cell membrane** is a thin boundary that surrounds a cell and protects its contents. It gives each cell its own separate identity, and it is also called the **plasma membrane**.
