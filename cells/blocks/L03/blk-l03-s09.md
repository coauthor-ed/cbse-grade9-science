# Three dishes

<!-- STEP -->
## Isotonic

Here the outside solution has the same solute concentration as the inside of the cells. It is an **isotonic** solution. The cells keep their size.

<!-- STEP -->
## Hypotonic

Here the outside solution has a lower solute concentration than the inside of the cells. It is a **hypotonic** solution, and the cells swell as water enters.

<!-- STEP -->
## Hypertonic

Here the outside solution has a higher solute concentration than the inside of the cells. It is a **hypertonic** solution, and the cells shrink as water leaves.

**The three words name the outside solution, compared with the cell. They do not name the cell.**
