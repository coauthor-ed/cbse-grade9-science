# What does the membrane let through?

<!-- STEP -->
## Some things pass

The cell membrane is **selectively permeable**, which means that it allows some substances to pass through it while it blocks others.

<!-- STEP -->
## In your lungs

You have already met an example. Oxygen and carbon dioxide cross the membranes of the alveoli in your lungs. The picture shows particles crossing a membrane, some through proteins set in it.

**A sealed bag would stop everything, and an open net would stop nothing. A cell membrane does neither.**
