# Water across a membrane

<!-- STEP -->
## Water crosses both ways

Two equal sugar solutions sit on either side of a selectively permeable membrane. Water crosses both ways all the time. The sugar never crosses, so its count on each side stays the same.

<!-- STEP -->
## More goes one way

Now there is more sugar on the right, and so less water there for each bit of sugar. Water passes to the right more often than it returns. The right side rises. This net movement of water through a selectively permeable membrane is **osmosis**.

<!-- STEP -->
## When the net flow stops

The right side gets more dilute as water arrives. When the two sides are equal, water still crosses, but equally both ways. Try the slider, then set the two sides the same.

**Osmosis is the diffusion of water across a selectively permeable membrane.**
