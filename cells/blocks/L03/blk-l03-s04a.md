# How do particles spread?

<!-- STEP -->
## Crowded in one corner

Light an agarbatti in the corner of a room. Near the stick the fragrance particles are crowded, and across the room there are none. This difference in concentration is called a **concentration gradient**.

<!-- STEP -->
## Net movement

Each particle moves at random. More move out of the crowded corner than into it, so there is a **net movement** from higher to lower concentration. This is **diffusion**, and it needs no membrane.

<!-- STEP -->
## Evenly spread

When the particles are evenly spread, they keep moving, but as many go one way as the other. The net movement is now zero, and nothing appears to change.

**Particles do not choose a direction. The net movement comes from the numbers on each side.**
