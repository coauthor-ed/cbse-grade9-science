## Ribosomes

**Ribosomes** are tiny structures that are the sites of protein synthesis. Some lie freely in the cytoplasm, and others are attached to a network called the endoplasmic reticulum. Press **Ribosomes**, **Rough ER** and **Smooth ER** to light each part.

<!-- STEP -->
## Rough Endoplasmic Reticulum

The **endoplasmic reticulum (ER)** is a large organelle that spreads like a network through the cytoplasm. The rough ER looks rough under an electron microscope because ribosomes are attached to its surface, and it is mainly involved in protein synthesis and secretion.

<!-- STEP -->
## Smooth Endoplasmic Reticulum

The smooth ER has no ribosomes on its surface, so it looks smooth. It is involved in the synthesis and storage of fats and hormones.

<!-- STEP -->
## One Connected Network

The ER is continuous with the outer membrane of the nuclear envelope. Its structure varies with the function of the cell: gland cells that secrete proteins, such as pancreatic cells, have a lot of rough ER.
