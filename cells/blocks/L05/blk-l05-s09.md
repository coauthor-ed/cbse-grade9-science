## Stacks of Flattened Sacs

Look at the pink stack in this cell. These flattened, sac-like structures form the **Golgi apparatus**. It is functionally linked to the ER, the cell membrane and other organelles.

<!-- STEP -->
## Like a Post Office

Your book says the Golgi apparatus acts like the post office of the cell, because it **modifies, sorts and packages** proteins and lipids into **vesicles**. A vesicle is a small membrane sac. Vesicles are used for transport, for secretion, or to form lysosomes.

Unlike a post office, it reads no addresses and employs nobody. The comparison only tells you the kind of work it does.
