## Smaller Than Any Cell

Some infectious agents are not cells at all. **Viruses**, **viroids** and **prions** are acellular, which means "no cells", and they are too small to be seen under a light microscope.

| Agent | Made of |
|---|---|
| Virus | Some genetic material with a protein coat |
| Viroid | Genetic material with no protein coat |
| Prion | A misfolded protein, with no genetic material |

None of them has a cell membrane or cytoplasm, so none of them can be called a prokaryote.
