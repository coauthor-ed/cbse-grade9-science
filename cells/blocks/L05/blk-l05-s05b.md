## Fine Fibres in the Cytoplasm

The cytoplasm of a eukaryotic cell also holds a network of fine fibres, the **cytoskeleton**. It gives structural support, maintains the shape of the cell, and enables cell movement and transport inside the cell. You would need an electron microscope to see it as a separate part.

## Stored Materials

The cytoplasm can also store materials. Starch is stored in plant cells, and crystals of calcium oxalate or silica are stored in some plant cells. These stored materials are called **cell inclusions**.
