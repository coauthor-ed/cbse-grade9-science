## Worn-Out Parts

As a cell works, it produces waste materials, and its organelles become damaged or worn out. If all of this stayed in the cell, it would keep piling up. The dull part in this picture is a worn-out mitochondrion.

<!-- STEP -->
## A Sac of Enzymes

A **lysosome** is a sac surrounded by a single membrane and filled with enzymes. These enzymes can break down unwanted proteins, carbohydrates, fats and even damaged parts of the cell. Press **Join** in the picture.

<!-- STEP -->
## Broken Down and Used Again

The products of the breakdown are released into the cytoplasm, where they may be reused in other cell processes.

**A lysosome keeps the cell clean, and what it breaks down can be used again.**
