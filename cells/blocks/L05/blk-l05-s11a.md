## Made at the Rough ER

Follow one protein that a cell will send out. A ribosome on the rough ER makes it, and the protein passes into the ER.

<!-- STEP -->
## To the Golgi Apparatus

A small vesicle carries the protein from the ER to the Golgi apparatus. There the protein is modified and sorted.

<!-- STEP -->
## Packed in a New Vesicle

The Golgi apparatus packages the protein into a new vesicle, which moves through the cytoplasm towards the edge of the cell.

<!-- STEP -->
## Out Through the Plasma Membrane

The vesicle joins the **plasma membrane**, not the cell wall, and its contents are released outside the cell. The wall of a plant cell is further out, and it is permeable.

**Route: ribosome on rough ER, Golgi apparatus, vesicle, plasma membrane.**
