## Many Jobs at Once

A eukaryotic cell builds new materials, removes waste and releases energy, and it does all of this at the same time. Each process runs in its own organelle, independently of the others.

## A Tiny Living Factory

Your book compares a cell to a factory. On the left, each room has its own task: machines in one, packing in another, a store in a third. On the right, each organelle does a specific job too.

The comparison stops there. Nobody manages a cell, and no organelle knows what it is doing. The organelles simply work together, and in that way they carry out all the functions of a cell.
