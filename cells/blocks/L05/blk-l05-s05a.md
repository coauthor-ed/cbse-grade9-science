## Three Basic Parts

Most cells have three basic parts:

- A selectively permeable **plasma membrane** around the outside.
- A semi-fluid, jelly-like substance called **cytoplasm**, which fills the cell.
- A prominent **nucleus**, shown here in purple.

From this distance the cytoplasm looks almost empty, like clear water around a stone. That impression is wrong, and an electron microscope shows why.
