# A crust around the spring

<!-- STEP -->
## What the scientists found

Scientists from the Birbal Sahni Institute of Palaeosciences, Lucknow, studied these hot springs. They found that **calcium carbonate** forms rapidly around them. It is a hard white mineral, the same substance as chalk.

<!-- STEP -->
## What it may have done

These deposits may have protected early organic molecules from harmful radiation and extreme conditions. They may also have helped to form the first protective membrane, the barrier that defines a cell.

**The word "may" matters here: this is an idea with evidence, not a settled fact.**
