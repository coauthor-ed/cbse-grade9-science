# Four questions to keep in mind

<!-- STEP -->
## What we will answer

These four questions run through all of these lessons:

- Where does a cell come from?
- How have new tools helped us to understand the world beyond what the eye can see?
- How is the cell the structural and functional unit of life?
- How does a cell multiply?

We will come back to all four at the very end.

## The first problem

A cell is usually too small to be seen by the unaided eye. So how did anyone find out what is inside one? We start with that problem, and with the limit of your own eyes.
