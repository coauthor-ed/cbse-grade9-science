# Where did life begin?

<!-- STEP -->
## In water

It is widely accepted among scientists that life began in water. Many people then picture the open ocean, and that is a reasonable picture.

<!-- STEP -->
## Perhaps in small pools

Some researchers think that life may have begun in small water pools, where conditions kept changing, rather than in the oceans. Hot springs are one example of such a pool.

Nobody was there to see it happen, so scientists say "may have". They test the idea with evidence from places like Puga.
