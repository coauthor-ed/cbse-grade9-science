# How do many cells work together?

<!-- STEP -->
## Similar cells form a tissue

A group of similar cells that perform similar functions forms a **tissue**. The cells in this drawing lie side by side and do the same work.

<!-- STEP -->
## Tissues form an organ

Different tissues are organised to form an **organ**. A lung is an organ, and it holds several kinds of tissue.

<!-- STEP -->
## Organs form an organ system

Several organs work together to form an **organ system**. The nasal pores, nasal cavity, trachea and lungs form the respiratory system.

<!-- STEP -->
## The cell is still the unit

You might think that the organ system has now taken over the work. It has not: even inside tissues, organs and organ systems, the cell remains the basic unit of structure and function.

**Every level is built from cells.**
