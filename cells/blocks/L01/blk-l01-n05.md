# One cell, or millions?

<!-- STEP -->
## Every living thing

All living organisms are made up of cells, from the bacteria in a hot spring to a neem tree. The cell is the basic level at which life exists.

<!-- STEP -->
## One cell or many

Some organisms, such as bacteria or yeast, are just one cell. They are **unicellular**, and that one cell does everything needed to stay alive.

Others, such as plants, fish, birds and humans, are made of millions of cells that work together. They are **multicellular**.
