## The Bacterial Cell

The bacterial cell has a cell membrane, a cell wall and cytoplasm, so it is a complete living cell. Its genetic material lies in a region called the **nucleoid**, with no membrane around it. It has no membrane-bound organelles. The thin tail at one end is an appendage for locomotion: the cell uses it to move.

<!-- STEP -->
## The Plant and Animal Cells

Plant and animal cells both have a well-defined **nucleus**, enclosed by a membrane, and several membrane-bound organelles.

<!-- STEP -->
## One Clear Difference

The plant cell has a cell wall, and the animal cell does not. Many plant cells also have chloroplasts and one large central vacuole, which you can see here.

**So a bacterial cell differs from the other two in its nucleus and organelles, not in being without a membrane or genetic material.**
