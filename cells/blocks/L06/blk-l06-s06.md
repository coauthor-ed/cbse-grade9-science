## The House of Coded Instructions

The **nucleus** is covered by a double-layered **nuclear membrane**. Two layers may sound like a sealed box, but the covering has **pores**, which allow material to pass between the nucleus and the cytoplasm.

<!-- STEP -->
## A Dense Round Body

Inside the nucleus is the **nucleolus**, a dense round body. This is where ribosomal subunits are made. A subunit is one piece of a ribosome, not a whole one.

<!-- STEP -->
## Two Pieces, One Ribosome

Press **Send subunits** in the picture. The subunits leave the nucleus through the pores. In the cytoplasm, one large and one small subunit assemble to form a **ribosome**.

<!-- STEP -->
## An Entangled Mass of Threads

The rest of the nucleus is filled with **chromatin** material, which looks like an entangled mass of thin threads. These threads hold the coded instructions, so we look at them more closely next.
