## Down Into the Nucleus

In a cell that is not dividing, DNA is present as part of **chromatin** material. Press **Cell**, **Nucleus** and **Chromatin** to zoom in, and notice that nothing rod-shaped is visible yet.

<!-- STEP -->
## When the Cell Is About to Divide

Whenever a cell is about to divide, the chromatin material gets organised into **chromosomes**, which are visible as rod-shaped structures only at that time.

Chromatin and chromosomes are not two different materials. They are the same material, loose in one case and organised in the other. Press **Thread** and **Rod** to compare.

<!-- STEP -->
## DNA

Chromosomes are composed of **DNA** (deoxyribonucleic acid) and specific proteins. DNA carries the information for the inheritance of characters from parents to the next generation. Stretched out, the DNA of a single human cell would be about 2 metres long.

<!-- STEP -->
## Genes

The functional segments of DNA are called **genes**. Each lit band in the picture stands for one gene, drawn much larger than a real gene.
