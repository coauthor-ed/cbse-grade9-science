## A Cell With No Nucleus

You might expect every cell in your body to have a nucleus. A mature **red blood cell** in humans does not have one. This cell is called enucleate.

<!-- STEP -->
## More Room for Haemoglobin

With no nucleus, there is more space for **haemoglobin**, the substance that carries oxygen. So each cell can transport more oxygen to all the cells of the body. The red dots in the picture stand for haemoglobin.

<!-- STEP -->
## What It Costs

A cell without a nucleus cannot repair itself or divide. Its lifespan is short, so a red blood cell survives for only about **120 days**.

Are there other cells with no nucleus? Yes: bacterial cells have no nucleus enclosed by a membrane. Their DNA lies in the nucleoid.

**So having a nucleus is not the same as being a cell.**
