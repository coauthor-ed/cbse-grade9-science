# Lightning First, Thunder Later

<!-- STEP -->
## Flash, then sound

In a thunderstorm you see the lightning first and hear the thunder later.

Light reaches you almost at once. Sound is much slower.

<!-- STEP -->
## How far away?

Suppose the thunder comes 5 s after the flash.

Distance = 340 m/s × 5 s = 1700 m. The lightning struck about 1.7 km away.

<!-- STEP -->
## Try it with your teacher

A friend bursts a balloon on the far side of a big playground. Time how long it takes from seeing the burst to hearing the pop.

Then divide the distance by the time to find the speed of sound.
