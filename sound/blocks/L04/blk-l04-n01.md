# Two Knocks on a Fence

<!-- STEP -->
## Through steel and through air

You put your ear to a long steel fence. A friend, 340 m away, knocks on the fence with a metal object.

The sound reaches you through the steel, and also through the air.

<!-- STEP -->
## Two sounds

Through the steel: 340 m ÷ 5000 m/s = 0.068 s.

Through the air: 340 m ÷ 340 m/s = 1 s.

Two sounds must reach you at least 0.1 s apart to be heard separately. Here the gap is 1 s − 0.068 s = 0.932 s, so you hear two knocks.
