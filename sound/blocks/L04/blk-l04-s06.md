# The Speed of Sound

<!-- STEP -->
## One wavelength in one time period

Follow one crest. In one time period it moves forward exactly one wavelength.

So speed = distance ÷ time = λ ÷ T. The speed of sound is written v.

<!-- STEP -->
## Speed = wavelength × frequency

Since ν = 1 ÷ T, the speed is the wavelength multiplied by the frequency:

**v = λ × ν**

Speed is measured in metres per second (m/s).
