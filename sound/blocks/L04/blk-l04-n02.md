# Reading a Wave Graph

<!-- STEP -->
## Find the wavelength

This graph shows a sound wave in steel. From one crest to the next is 50 m, so λ = 50 m.

Sound travels through steel at 5000 m/s.

<!-- STEP -->
## Find the frequency and time period

ν = v ÷ λ = 5000 ÷ 50 = 100 Hz.

T = 1 ÷ ν = 1 ÷ 100 = 0.01 s.
