# Time Period

<!-- STEP -->
## The time for one oscillation

The time for one complete oscillation at a fixed place is the **time period**, T. It is measured in seconds (s).

The shorter the time period, the higher the frequency: ν = 1 ÷ T.

<!-- STEP -->
## An example

A source makes 10 oscillations in 2 s.

Frequency = 10 ÷ 2 s = 5 Hz.

Time period = 2 s ÷ 10 = 0.2 s.
