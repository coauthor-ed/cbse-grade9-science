# Same Air, Same Speed

<!-- STEP -->
## Raise the frequency

In air, the speed of sound depends on the air, not on the source.

Raise the frequency and the wavelength gets shorter. The speed stays the same.

<!-- STEP -->
## An example

We hear sounds from about 20 Hz to 20 000 Hz. In air, sound travels at about 344 m/s.

For 20 Hz: λ = 344 ÷ 20 = 17.2 m.

For 20 000 Hz: λ = 344 ÷ 20 000 = 0.0172 m, which is 1.72 cm.
