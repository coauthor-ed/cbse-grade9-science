# Wavelength

<!-- STEP -->
## Crest to crest

The distance from one crest to the next, or from one trough to the next, is the **wavelength**.

It is written λ (the Greek letter lambda) and measured in metres (m).

<!-- STEP -->
## Long and short

Compare two waves. When the crests are far apart, the wavelength is long. When the crests are close together, the wavelength is short.

A crest and the next trough are half a wavelength apart.
