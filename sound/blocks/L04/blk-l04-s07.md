# Solids, Liquids, Gases

<!-- STEP -->
## A race

One knock travels through steel, water and air at the same time.

Sound is fastest in solids, slower in liquids and slowest in gases.

<!-- STEP -->
## The speeds

At 15 °C, sound travels at about 5000 m/s in steel, 1500 m/s in water and 340 m/s in air.

In water, sound travels about 4.4 times faster than in air. In steel, it travels about 3.3 times faster than in water.

<!-- STEP -->
## Warmer air

In air, the speed of sound also rises with temperature and humidity. It is about 331 m/s at 0 °C, and nearly 344 m/s at 22 °C.
