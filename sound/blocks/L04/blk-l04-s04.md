# Frequency

<!-- STEP -->
## One complete oscillation

Stand at one fixed place. As compressions and rarefactions pass, the density there goes up and down.

From maximum density to minimum and back to maximum is one complete oscillation.

<!-- STEP -->
## Oscillations per second

The number of oscillations per second at a fixed place is the **frequency**. It is written ν (the Greek letter nu, not the letter v).

Its unit is per second, called a **hertz** (Hz). The wave with crests close together has the higher frequency, so the answer is b.
