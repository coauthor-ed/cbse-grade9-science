# Amplitude

<!-- STEP -->
## A small change and a big change

Here are two sound waves with the same wavelength. In one, the density changes only a little from the average. In the other, it changes a lot.

The **amplitude** is the biggest change in density from the average, in a compression or a rarefaction.

<!-- STEP -->
## More energy

A wave with a larger amplitude carries more energy.

Beat the plate harder, and the particles of the air move further from their places. The sheet on the bowl vibrates more, and the grains jump higher.
