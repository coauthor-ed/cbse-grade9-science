# Sa, Re, Ga

<!-- STEP -->
## Notes that rise

Sing Sa, Re, Ga, Ma, Pa, Dha, Ni, Sa, or play them on a phone. A free app such as Phyphox can show the frequency of each note.

Sa has the lowest frequency, and each note after it has a higher frequency.

<!-- STEP -->
## One frequency or many

A tuning fork, or whistling with your lips, makes a sound of almost one single frequency.

Most sounds around you are a mixture of many frequencies.
