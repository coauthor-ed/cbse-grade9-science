# Inside the Ear

<!-- STEP -->
## Eardrum, bones, cochlea

Sound enters the ear and makes a thin membrane, the **eardrum**, vibrate. Tiny bones make the vibrations larger.

The **cochlea** turns them into electrical signals, which travel to the brain.

<!-- STEP -->
## Two ears

Two ears help the brain find where a sound comes from. It compares which ear hears the sound first.

The difference is often less than a thousandth of a second.

<!-- STEP -->
## Other animals

Snakes and fish sense vibrations through their bodies. Some insects have ear-like organs on parts of their bodies.
