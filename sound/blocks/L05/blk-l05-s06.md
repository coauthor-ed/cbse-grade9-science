# Decibels and Noise

<!-- STEP -->
## Measuring in decibels

Loudness is often measured in **decibels** (dB). Rustling leaves are a few dB. Normal talk is about 60 dB. Firecrackers are over 100 dB.

A small rise in decibels means a large rise in intensity.

<!-- STEP -->
## Noise

Unwanted or harmful sound is called **noise**. Long exposure to loud sound can harm your health, your sleep and your hearing, and can cause hearing loss.

Hearing is tested with an audiogram. A hearing aid has a microphone, an amplifier and a speaker: it makes sounds louder for the listener.
