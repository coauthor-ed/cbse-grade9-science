# Loudness

<!-- STEP -->
## Amplitude and loudness

We hear the amplitude of a sound as its **loudness**. A larger amplitude sounds louder. A smaller amplitude sounds softer.

The loudness falls as you move away from the source.

<!-- STEP -->
## Not the same as intensity

People often mix up loudness and intensity. Intensity can be measured.

Loudness depends on the listener's hearing, so the same sound can seem louder to one person than to another.
