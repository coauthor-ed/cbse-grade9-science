# Timbre

<!-- STEP -->
## Same note, different sound

A flute, an ektara and a tabla play the same note, equally loudly. They still sound different.

This quality is called **timbre**. It comes from each instrument's shape, material and construction, which set its overtones.

<!-- STEP -->
## Richer sounds

The sarangi, sitar and veena have extra strings that make the sound richer. The tabla and the mridangam have a black circle of paste on the drum skin, the syaahi. It changes how the skin vibrates.

<!-- STEP -->
## C. V. Raman

C. V. Raman, who won India's first Nobel Prize in Science for his work on light, also studied the tabla and the mridangam to understand their rich sounds.
