# Pitch

<!-- STEP -->
## Faster vibration, higher pitch

The thin band vibrates more times each second: it has a higher frequency.

We hear frequency as **pitch**. A higher frequency gives a higher, shriller pitch. So the answer is b.

<!-- STEP -->
## High and low

A whistle or a siren has a high pitch. Thunder, or the rumble of an aircraft, has a low pitch.

In general, a high pitch goes with a high frequency, though the exact link is complicated.

<!-- STEP -->
## Voices change

In adolescence, boys' vocal cords grow longer and thicker, and vibrate fewer times each second.

Their voices get deeper.
