# What Humans Hear

<!-- STEP -->
## The audible range

Your teacher plays a sound of 100 Hz, then 200 Hz, and so on up to 1000 Hz: the pitch rises. Then 50 Hz, and down to about 20 Hz, where the sound fades away.

Humans hear from about 20 Hz to 20 000 Hz (20 kHz). This is the **audible range**. It differs from person to person, and it narrows with age.

<!-- STEP -->
## Below and above

Sound below 20 Hz is **infrasonic**. Sound above 20 kHz is **ultrasonic**. Humans cannot hear them, but some animals can.

Dogs, cats, bats and dolphins can detect ultrasound. Elephants can detect infrasound.
