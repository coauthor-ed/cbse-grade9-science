# Tone and Note

<!-- STEP -->
## A tone

A tuning fork, or a person whistling, makes a **tone**: a sound of a single frequency.

Its graph is a smooth curve that repeats.

<!-- STEP -->
## A note

A sung note, or a plucked tanpura string, is a **note**: a mixture of frequencies. The lowest frequency is the **fundamental**. The higher ones are **overtones**.

Its curve is more jagged, but it still repeats.

<!-- STEP -->
## An octave

Two notes where one has double the frequency of the other are an **octave** apart. For example, 200 Hz and 400 Hz are an octave apart.
