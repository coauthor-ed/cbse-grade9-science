## Parking sensors

A car's parking sensor sends out ultrasound at about 40 kHz. Suppose the warning starts when the car is 1.2 m from an obstacle.

The sound goes 2.4 m, out and back: 2.4 m ÷ 345 m/s = about 0.007 s. The echo comes back almost at once, so the warning is quick.

## Listening for drones

Drones and aircraft hum from their motors. Even when they are hard to see, sensitive sound sensors can detect the low hum.

This is called audio surveillance.
