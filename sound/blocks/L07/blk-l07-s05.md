# Uses Below and Above

<!-- STEP -->
## Infrasonic waves

Infrasonic waves, below 20 Hz, travel long distances through the air and through the Earth.

They are used to detect earthquakes, volcanic eruptions and severe storms.

<!-- STEP -->
## Ultrasonic waves

Ultrasonic waves, above 20 kHz, are used to:

- see organs inside the body without surgery (ultrasonography)
- break kidney stones
- weld and clean delicate machine parts
- find defects inside metal blocks
- find objects by their echoes
