## There and back

The 2.0 s is the time to go down and come back. The way down took 1.0 s, so the depth is 1500 m/s × 1.0 s = 1500 m. So the answer is b.

The frequency does not matter: in one medium, all frequencies travel at the same speed.

## Sound navigation and ranging

This is **sonar**: sound navigation and ranging. A ship sends ultrasound into the water and studies the echoes.

From the echoes it finds the distance, direction and speed of underwater objects, such as submarines and shipwrecks.
