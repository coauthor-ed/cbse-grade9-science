# Bats Find Prey by Echo

<!-- STEP -->
## Bursts of ultrasound

Most bats send out short bursts of ultrasound as they fly at night. The bursts reflect off nearby objects.

By sensing the echoes, the bat finds where obstacles and prey are.

<!-- STEP -->
## Echolocation

This is **echolocation**: finding objects using reflected sound. Dolphins, whales and some birds use it too.

This answers the bat question from the start of the course.
