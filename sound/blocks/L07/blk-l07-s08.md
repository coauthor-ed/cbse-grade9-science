# Sound Explores

<!-- STEP -->
## Mars and the ocean

Sound helps us explore beyond our hearing. Space probes have recorded the first sounds from Mars.

Scientists time the sound of distant earthquakes to measure tiny changes in the temperature of the ocean.

<!-- STEP -->
## Mosquitoes and soil

Biologists use the buzz of mosquitoes to identify the ones that carry disease.

Researchers listen to the tiny crackles of microbes in soil to study the health of the soil.
