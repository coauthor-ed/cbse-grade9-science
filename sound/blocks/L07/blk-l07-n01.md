## The two questions answered

The astronauts: space is nearly a vacuum, and sound needs a medium, so they cannot hear each other directly.

The bats: they send out ultrasound and find their prey by its echoes.

<!-- STEP -->
## What you learned

Sound is made by vibrating objects. It travels as a longitudinal mechanical wave of compressions and rarefactions.

Wavelength, frequency, time period, amplitude and speed describe a sound wave: v = λ × ν. An echo needs a gap of at least 0.1 s. Below 20 Hz is infrasonic; above 20 kHz is ultrasonic.
