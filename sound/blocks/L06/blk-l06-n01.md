# Reverberation

<!-- STEP -->
## Many reflections

In a big hall, sound reflects many times off the walls, the ceiling and the floor. The reflections keep the sound going after the source stops.

This is **reverberation**. It happens when the reflections reach you very close together, less than 0.05 s apart, so you cannot hear them separately.

<!-- STEP -->
## The right amount

Halls are designed to have the right amount of reverberation. Too much makes speech and music hard to understand.

Sound-absorbing panels, cushioned chairs and curtains cut unwanted reverberation.
