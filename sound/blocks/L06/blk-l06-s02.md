# The 0.1 s Rule

<!-- STEP -->
## Two reflections

Both the wall and the cliff reflect your clap. From the wall, the sound comes back in about 0.01 s and mixes with your clap.

From the far cliff, it comes back after about a second, and you hear it again. This is an **echo**. So the answer is b.

<!-- STEP -->
## At least 0.1 s apart

You hear two sounds separately only if they reach you at least 0.1 s apart.
