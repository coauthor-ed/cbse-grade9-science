# Through a Desk

<!-- STEP -->
## Through the air

You and a friend stand at the two ends of a desk. Your friend gently scratches the desk with a finger.

You hear the scratch through the air.

<!-- STEP -->
## Through the wood

Now press one ear to the desk and close your other ear with your hand. Your friend scratches again.

The scratch sounds clearer. The sound travelled through the solid wood.
