# The Bell Jar

<!-- STEP -->
## Pumping out the air

An electric bell rings inside a glass jar. A vacuum pump slowly draws the air out of the jar.

The sound gets fainter and fainter.

<!-- STEP -->
## Almost no air

With almost no air left, you can see the hammer striking the bell, but you hear almost nothing. Let the air back in, and the sound slowly returns.

**Sound needs a medium to travel. It cannot travel through a vacuum.**

<!-- STEP -->
## Back to the astronauts

Space is nearly a vacuum. So the astronauts cannot hear each other directly, and the answer is c.

They talk to each other through special devices fitted in their spacesuits.
