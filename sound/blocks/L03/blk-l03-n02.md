# Bangs and Booms

<!-- STEP -->
## A sudden bang

Firecrackers and thunder make sudden loud sounds. Air or gas heated very fast expands very fast. This sudden change in air density travels outward. When it reaches your ears, you hear a loud sound pulse.

<!-- STEP -->
## A sonic boom

A supersonic aircraft flies faster than sound. It makes a similar but more complex disturbance in the air. We hear it as a loud **sonic boom**.
