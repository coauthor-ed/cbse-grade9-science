# The Particles Stay in Place

<!-- STEP -->
## Again and again

Move the piston forward and back again and again. Compressions and rarefactions travel down the tube one after another.

Watch the red particle. It moves to and fro about its own place. It never travels with them.

<!-- STEP -->
## A sound wave

**A sound wave is a series of compressions and rarefactions travelling through a medium, while the particles only vibrate about their own places.**

The way the wave travels is called the **direction of propagation**.

<!-- STEP -->
## So what reached your ear?

Not the air from beside the fork. Not its metal. Not a stream of air.

The energy carried by the sound reached your ear, so the answer is b. The particles beside your ear vibrate, and your ear picks up that vibration.
