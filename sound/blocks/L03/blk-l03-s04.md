# A Slinky Model

<!-- STEP -->
## One push, one pull

Lay a slinky, a long spring toy, on the floor. A friend holds the far end still. Mark one turn with red.

Push your end sharply forward, then pull it back. Watch what runs along the slinky.

<!-- STEP -->
## Again and again

Push and pull again and again.

Regions where the turns are close together, and regions where they are spread out, travel along the slinky one after another.

<!-- STEP -->
## Watch the red turn

Now watch only the red turn. It moves a little to and fro about its own place.

**The regions travel along the slinky. The red turn does not travel with them.**
