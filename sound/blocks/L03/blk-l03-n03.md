# Microphone and Speaker

<!-- STEP -->
## A microphone

A microphone changes sound energy into electrical energy. Sound waves make a thin sheet inside it vibrate. This sheet is called the **diaphragm**. Its vibrations are changed into an electrical signal.

<!-- STEP -->
## A speaker

A speaker does the opposite. An electrical signal makes a cone inside the speaker vibrate, and the cone makes sound. If everything works well, the sound from the speaker closely matches the sound the microphone picked up.
