# Longitudinal and Mechanical

<!-- STEP -->
## Along the same line

In a sound wave, the particles vibrate back and forth along the same line that the wave travels. They vibrate parallel to the direction of propagation.

A wave like this is called a **longitudinal wave**. Sound is a longitudinal wave.

<!-- STEP -->
## It needs a medium

Without particles, nothing can pass the vibration on. So sound cannot travel through a vacuum.

A wave that needs a material medium to travel is called a **mechanical wave**. Sound is a mechanical wave.
