# Across, Not Along

<!-- STEP -->
## A wave on a rope

Shake one end of a long rope up and down. A wave travels along the rope. But each point of the rope only moves up and down, across the way the wave travels.

When the particles vibrate perpendicular to the direction of propagation, the wave is a **transverse wave**.

<!-- STEP -->
## Earthquakes send both

An earthquake sends both kinds of waves through the Earth. A seismograph records them. The longitudinal waves reach it first. The transverse waves arrive later.

<!-- STEP -->
## Light needs no medium

Light is a transverse wave too, but it is not a mechanical wave. It needs no medium. Light from the Sun and the stars crosses the vacuum of space to reach us.
