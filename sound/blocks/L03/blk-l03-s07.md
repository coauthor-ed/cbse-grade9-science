# Sound Moves Grains

<!-- STEP -->
## Nothing touches the bowl

Stretch a sheet of balloon rubber over a wide bowl and sprinkle some rice grains on it.

Make a loud sound near the bowl by beating a metal plate. Do not touch the bowl.

<!-- STEP -->
## The grains jump

The grains jump. The sound reached the sheet through the air and made it vibrate.

**In a sound wave, energy travels, not the particles of the medium.** Sound is a form of energy.

<!-- STEP -->
## Beat harder

Beat the plate harder. The grains jump higher.

A harder beat gives more energy to the air, and more energy reaches the sheet.
