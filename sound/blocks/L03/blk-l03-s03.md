# A Tube of Air

<!-- STEP -->
## Air at rest

Air is made of tiny particles. Picture a long tube of air with a piston at one end. Each dot stands for many air particles.

At rest the particles are spread evenly. How closely they are packed is called the **density**. Here every part of the tube has the same density: the **average density**.

<!-- STEP -->
## Push: a compression

Push the piston forward. The air next to it is packed closer than average. This region is a **compression (C)**.

These particles push on the particles ahead of them, and those push on the next. So the compression moves along the tube.

<!-- STEP -->
## Pull: a rarefaction

Pull the piston back. The air next to it spreads out, so it is less packed than average. This region is a **rarefaction (R)**.

The rarefaction also moves along the tube, behind the compression.
