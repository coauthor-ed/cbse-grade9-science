# The Tuning Fork

<!-- STEP -->
## Stem and prongs

A **tuning fork** is a U-shaped metal bar with a stem, usually made of steel or aluminium.

The two sides of the U are its **prongs**, also called tines.

<!-- STEP -->
## Strike it gently

Hold the fork by its stem. Strike one prong gently on a rubber pad, not on a hard surface.

Bring it near your ear. You hear a clear, steady sound.
