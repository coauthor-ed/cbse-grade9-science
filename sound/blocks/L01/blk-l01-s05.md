# Many Sources

<!-- STEP -->
## Something always vibrates

Pluck a string, beat a drum, blow into a bansuri, strike a taal. Each time, something vibrates.

Sound can come from vibrating strings, stretched skins (membranes), columns of air and many other objects.

<!-- STEP -->
## Air and metal

In a bansuri nothing solid moves: the air inside the hollow pipe vibrates.

A metal like the taal rings when it is struck. You met this as **sonority** in Grade 7.

Most instruments have more than one vibrating part.
