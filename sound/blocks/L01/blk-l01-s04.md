# Tight, Loose, and the Box

<!-- STEP -->
## Tighter and looser

Stretch the band tighter and pluck it again: the sound changes. Loosen it: the sound changes again.

The band vibrates faster when it is tighter.

<!-- STEP -->
## Without the box

Now take the band off the box. Hold it stretched between two fingers near your ear and pluck it. You still hear it, but more softly.

The box and the air inside it vibrate too, and make the sound louder.
