# Your Voice, and a Cricket

<!-- STEP -->
## Feel your throat

Touch your throat while you hum. You feel a buzz.

Inside your voice box, the **larynx**, are two **vocal cords**: tightly stretched muscular flaps.

<!-- STEP -->
## Open and closed

When you breathe, the vocal cords are open. When you speak or sing, they close up, and air from the lungs makes them vibrate.

Your tongue, lips, mouth and nose shape the sound into speech.

<!-- STEP -->
## Rubbing body parts

Some animals make sound by rubbing body parts together. Crickets rub their wings. Grasshoppers rub their legs against their wings.
