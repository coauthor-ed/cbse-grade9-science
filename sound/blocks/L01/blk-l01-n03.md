# Kongthong: Names Made of Sound

<!-- STEP -->
## The Whistling Village

In Kongthong, a village near Shillong in Meghalaya, every person has a tune name. It can be sung or whistled across the hills.

Kongthong is also called the Whistling Village.

<!-- STEP -->
## A tune from birth

The tradition is called Jingrwai Iawbei. It begins at birth, when a mother composes a tune, like a lullaby, for her child.
