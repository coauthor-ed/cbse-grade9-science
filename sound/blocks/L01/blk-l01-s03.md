# Sound Comes From Vibration

<!-- STEP -->
## Watch until it stops

Watch the band until it stops. The sound fades as the movement fades, and stops when the band stops.

The band is still stretched, and your finger is long gone. So the answer is b: the sound lasts as long as the band goes on moving to and fro.

<!-- STEP -->
## Vibration

**Sound is produced by vibration.**

A vibration is a to-and-fro movement, repeated again and again. One to-and-fro movement is called an oscillation.

<!-- STEP -->
## The source

The object that vibrates and makes the sound is the **source** of the sound.

Here the source is the rubber band.
