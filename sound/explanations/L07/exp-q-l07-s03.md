# Two Echoes, Step by Step

<!-- STEP -->
## Step 1: halve the echo time

The echo time is the time to go down and come back. At the new place, the echo returns after 1.6 s.

Time to go down = 1.6 s ÷ 2 = **0.8 s**

<!-- STEP -->
## Step 2: find the depth

Depth = 1500 m/s × 0.8 s = **1200 m**

<!-- STEP -->
## Step 3: the first place

At the first place: 2.4 s ÷ 2 = 1.2 s, and 1500 m/s × 1.2 s = 1800 m.

So the sea is shallower at the new place.
