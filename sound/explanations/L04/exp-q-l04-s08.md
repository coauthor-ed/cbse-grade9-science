# Two Arrivals, Step by Step

<!-- STEP -->
## Step 1: through the steel

The fence carries the sound for the whole 170 m.

- distance = 170 m
- speed in steel = 5000 m/s
- time = 170 ÷ 5000 = **0.034 s**

<!-- STEP -->
## Step 2: through the air

The sound also travels 170 m through the air.

- distance = 170 m
- speed in air = 340 m/s
- time = 170 ÷ 340 = **0.5 s**

<!-- STEP -->
## Step 3: the gap

The sound through the air arrives later.

gap = 0.5 − 0.034 = **0.466 s**

This is more than 0.1 s, so you hear two separate knocks.
