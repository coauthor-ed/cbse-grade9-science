# No Machine Runs for Ever

<!-- STEP -->
## Losses can shrink, not vanish

Smooth bearings and oil make friction smaller. They cannot make it zero. Air resistance is never zero either.

## Every turn costs a little

At every turn, some mechanical energy becomes heat and sound. Nothing puts it back. So the machine slows down and stops, however well it is made.

A machine that also lifts a load needs even more energy, and it has no source for it.
