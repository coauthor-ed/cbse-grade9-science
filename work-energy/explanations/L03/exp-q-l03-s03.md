# The Jet, Step by Step

<!-- STEP -->
## Start from the end

The jet ends at rest, so its final kinetic energy is zero. The wire's work takes away all the kinetic energy the jet had when it landed.

<!-- STEP -->
## The sign of the wire's work

The jet moves forward along the deck. The wire pulls back. Force and displacement are opposite, so the wire does negative work on the jet:

$$W = -367500\ \mathrm{N} \times 100\ \mathrm{m} = -36\,750\,000\ \mathrm{J}$$

<!-- STEP -->
## Find the speed

Work done = change in kinetic energy, so the jet's kinetic energy at landing was 36 750 000 J:

$$\tfrac{1}{2} \times 15000 \times v^2 = 36\,750\,000$$

$$v^2 = \frac{2 \times 36\,750\,000}{15000} = 4900, \quad v = 70\ \mathrm{m/s}$$
