# Sorting the Seven

## Ask what the energy does

Each name says what the energy does:

- makes a thing warm: **thermal**
- lets you see: **light**
- travels as vibrations through a material: **sound**
- comes from charges: **electrical**
- is held in the nuclei of atoms: **nuclear**
- is held in the bonds between atoms, in fuel and food: **chemical**

**Mechanical** energy is different. It is the energy an object has because of where it is and how it moves.

## Two that get mixed up

Thermal energy has made something warmer, like a cup of hot tea. Sound energy is carried by vibrations, like the ring of a bell.
