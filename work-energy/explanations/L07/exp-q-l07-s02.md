# Finding the Effort

<!-- STEP -->
## The load

The load is the drum's weight: $mg = 50 \times 10 = 500$ N.

## The ramp's mechanical advantage

$$MA = \frac{L}{h} = \frac{4}{1} = 4$$

So the effort is $500 \div 4 = 125$ N.

## Check with work

Straight up: 500 N $\times$ 1 m = 500 J. Up the ramp: 125 N $\times$ 4 m = 500 J. The force is a quarter, the distance is four times as long, and the work is the same.
