# The Stone in the Sand

<!-- STEP -->
## At the top

The stone starts at rest, so it has no kinetic energy. All its energy is potential energy:

$$U = mgh = 2 \times 10 \times 5 = 100\ \mathrm{J}$$

## Just before the sand

At the ground, $U = 0$. All 100 J has become kinetic energy.

## In the sand

The sand pushes back against the motion until the stone stops. To stop it, the sand must take away all 100 J. So the sand does 100 J of work on the stone, negative in sign.

A heavier stone dropped from the same height has more energy, so it makes a deeper dent.
