# The Crowbar Does Not Make Work

<!-- STEP -->
## The true part

The crowbar does give a larger force at the rock than your hand gives. That is why we use it.

## The wrong step

A larger force does not mean more work. Work is force $\times$ distance, at each end:

$$F_1 \times d_1 = F_2 \times d_2$$

Your end moves a long way with a small force. The rock moves a short way with a large force. Ignoring friction, the two products are equal.

**A lever reduces the force needed, not the work.**
