# Same Energy, Different Speeds

<!-- STEP -->
## Equal kinetic energy

Object A has mass $m$. Object B has mass $4m$. They have the same kinetic energy.

<!-- STEP -->
## Half the speed

Then B must move at half the speed of A:

$\tfrac{1}{2}(4m)\left(\tfrac{v}{2}\right)^2 = \tfrac{1}{2}mv^2$.

The speeds of A and B are in the ratio 2 : 1.
