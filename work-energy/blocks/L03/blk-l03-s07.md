# Stopping a Jet

<!-- STEP -->
## A wire on the deck

A jet of mass 15000 kg lands on the deck of a ship. A hook on the jet catches a wire. The wire pulls back with a force of 367500 N and stops the jet in 100 m.

<!-- STEP -->
## Negative work

The wire pulls back while the jet moves forward, so the wire does negative work: $-367500 \times 100 = -36\,750\,000$ J.

This work takes away all the jet's kinetic energy. So $\tfrac{1}{2} \times 15000 \times v^2 = 36\,750\,000$.

<!-- STEP -->
## The landing speed

So $v^2 = 4900$ and $v = 70$ m/s. That is 252 km/h.
