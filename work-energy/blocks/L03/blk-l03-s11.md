# Steady Speed, Steady Energy

<!-- STEP -->
## Same at every point

A car moves at a steady speed on a level road. Its kinetic energy is the same as it passes a tree, and the same as it passes a petrol pump.

Kinetic energy depends on mass and speed, not on where the object is.

<!-- STEP -->
## Energy without moving

A ball held up on a high shelf does not move, so it has no kinetic energy. Yet if it falls, it can do work.

The next lesson measures that energy.
