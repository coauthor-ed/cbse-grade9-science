# A Fast Delivery

<!-- STEP -->
## Change the unit first

An Indian bowler once delivered a 0.2 kg cricket ball at about 154.8 km/h.

To use $K = \tfrac{1}{2}mv^2$, the speed must be in m/s. 1 km = 1000 m and 1 h = 3600 s, so

154.8 km/h = 154.8 $\times$ 1000 / 3600 = 43 m/s.

<!-- STEP -->
## The kinetic energy

$K = \tfrac{1}{2} \times 0.2 \times 43^2 = 184.9$ J.

Your textbook writes the unit m/s as m s$^{-1}$. Both mean metres per second.
