# Work Becomes Kinetic Energy

<!-- STEP -->
## A push on a trolley

Start with a small example. A 2 kg trolley is at rest on a smooth floor. You push it with a steady force of 9 N for 1 m.

Work done = 9 N $\times$ 1 m = 9 J.

<!-- STEP -->
## The trolley's energy

By the work-energy theorem, the trolley's energy rises by 9 J. It started at rest, so it now has 9 J of kinetic energy.

It now moves at 3 m/s. The next slide shows why.
