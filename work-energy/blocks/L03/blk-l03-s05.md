# Double the Speed

<!-- STEP -->
## The speed is squared

Double the speed of the car: $\tfrac{1}{2}m(2v)^2 = 4 \times \tfrac{1}{2}mv^2$.

**Twice the speed gives four times the kinetic energy.**

<!-- STEP -->
## Double the mass

Now double the mass instead, at the same speed. The kinetic energy is only twice as large.

The speed is squared in $K = \tfrac{1}{2}mv^2$, but the mass is not.
