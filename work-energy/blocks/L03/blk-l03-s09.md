# Challenge: Load the Launcher

<!-- STEP -->
## Push the crate, land on the number

A crate sits on a frictionless floor with a target energy pinned above it. You choose how hard to push and how far to push along the track, then commit.

The run posts one number when it finishes: what the crate is carrying. **Hit the target exactly and it counts.** Stop short, and the panel tells you how short. Sail past it, and the run is wasted.

<!-- STEP -->
## There are two ways to land on the number

The obvious way is to push hard enough and long enough that the crate arrives carrying the target.

The other way uses the negative work from lesson 1. Push the crate along, and then **push back against it** for the rest of the run. Negative work is still work, and it takes energy out just as positive work puts it in.

A run that overshoots and is then slowed back down onto the target is a legitimate win. Braking is not a penalty here.

<!-- STEP -->
## Work it out first

The crate's energy is worked out from its own mass and speed at the end of every run. Hit the target and it is because $\frac{1}{2}mv^2$ came out right, either going in or coming back out.
