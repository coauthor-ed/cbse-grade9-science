# A Car Brakes

<!-- STEP -->
## Steady, then stopping

A car of mass 1000 kg moves at a steady 35 m/s. The driver sees traffic ahead. For 1 s, while the driver reacts, the car keeps its speed (A to B). Then the brakes stop it in 2 s (B to C).

<!-- STEP -->
## Where the energy goes

At A, the car's kinetic energy is $\tfrac{1}{2} \times 1000 \times 35^2 = 612\,500$ J.

Between B and C, the brakes do $-612\,500$ J of work. The kinetic energy becomes heat in the brakes, tyres and road, and some sound.
