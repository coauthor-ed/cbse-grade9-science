# The Gharat

<!-- STEP -->
## A water mill in the hills

In the Himalayan region, some villages grind grain in a water mill called a gharat, or panchakki. Many can still be found in hilly regions.

<!-- STEP -->
## How it works

The water starts high up with potential energy. As it comes down the pipe (A), this becomes kinetic energy. The moving water drives the wheel (B) and makes it turn. The wheel turns the grinding stone above it (C).

<!-- STEP -->
## Dams today

Dams do the same today. The potential energy of the water stored in a dam becomes kinetic energy, which is used to make electricity.
