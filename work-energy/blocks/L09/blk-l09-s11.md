## Three slides, one speed

Back to the three slides from the start of the course. Without friction, every child reaches the bottom at $v = \sqrt{2gh}$, whatever the slide's shape and whatever the child's mass.

## Real slides

Real slides lose a little energy to friction, so real speeds are a bit lower.

The energy is not lost. It changes form, into heat and sound.
