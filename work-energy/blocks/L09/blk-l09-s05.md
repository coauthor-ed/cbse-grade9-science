## Work and energy

Work = force $\times$ displacement: $W = F \times s$.

Energy is the capacity to do work. Work done on an object = change in its energy.

<!-- STEP -->
## The formulas

$K = \tfrac{1}{2}mv^2$. $U = mgh$. $P = \dfrac{W}{t}$.

Simple machines change the size or direction of a force. They do not reduce the total work.
