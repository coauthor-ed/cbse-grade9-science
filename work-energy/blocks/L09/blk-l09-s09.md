# Challenge: Balance the Energy

<!-- STEP -->
## A machine, a fixed amount in, and two ways to spend it

Each round gives you a machine - a pulley, a ramp or a lever - and a fixed amount of work to put into it.

The output side has two rows: the work the machine actually handed to the load, and the heat and sound it gave to everything else.

**Split the input between them so the books close exactly.**

<!-- STEP -->
## Two ways to fail, and one of them is the important one

**Output over input.** The panel refuses the round. No machine hands back more work than it was given.

**Books that do not close.** Even with the output under the input, the two rows have to add back to the number that went in. Every joule of it has to be somewhere.

Every joule has to be somewhere: useful work, or heat and sound.

<!-- STEP -->
## Work it out first

A round clears only when the input is fully accounted for: useful work out, and heat and sound for the rest. The machine changes which split is easy. It never changes the total.
