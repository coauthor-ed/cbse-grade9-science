# Try It Yourself

<!-- STEP -->
## A refill launcher

Make a launcher for an empty pen refill with a rubber band. Stretch the band by different amounts and see how far the refill goes. Is there a link?

<!-- STEP -->
## Build a machine

Build a lever, a pulley or a ramp from rulers, bottle caps, thread and paper cups. Measure the effort and the load, and find the mechanical advantage.
