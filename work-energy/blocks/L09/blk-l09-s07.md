# The Verdict, on the Crank

<!-- STEP -->
## A hundred turns, accounted for

The crank again. A hundred turns of input, and the output meter stops short.

**Not once, and not on the last turn. Every single time.**

The difference went into friction at the pivot, into the air, into sound, into the heat of the bearings. The panel has been counting it the whole time.

<!-- STEP -->
## And the three machines, one line each

A **pulley** shares a load between ropes. A **ramp** trades a push for a longer push. A **lever** trades a push for a shorter push.

Three trades, one product, and not one of them came out ahead of what went in.

**Machines do not create energy. They help us use it more effectively.**

That took eight lessons to earn.

<!-- STEP -->
## The crank was never a mystery

Nobody has ever built a machine that runs for ever. The reason is not a bad design: the rule applies to every machine, however clever.

**Every crank ever turned lost a little. The heat meter was always there.**
