# Carrying a Box

<!-- STEP -->
## Up and across

A girl carries a box as she walks across a room. She holds it up with an upward force. The box moves sideways.

<!-- STEP -->
## At right angles

Her force and the box's displacement are at right angles. The box has no displacement in the direction of her force.

**So her force does no work on the box.**

Forces at other angles are studied in higher classes.
