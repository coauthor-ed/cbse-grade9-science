# Positive and Negative Work

<!-- STEP -->
## Same direction

A boy pushes a wheelchair forward. His force and the wheelchair's displacement point the same way.

The work done by his force is positive.

<!-- STEP -->
## Opposite directions

A goalkeeper stops a ball. Her force on the ball points back, but the ball still moves forward while she stops it.

Force and displacement are opposite, so the work done by her force on the ball is negative.

<!-- STEP -->
## Just a number

**Work has no direction. It is a number: positive, negative or zero.**
