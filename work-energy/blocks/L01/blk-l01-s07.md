# The Joule

<!-- STEP -->
## The unit of work

Work is measured in joules (J).

1 J is the work done when a force of 1 N moves an object by 1 m in the direction of the force. So 1 J = 1 N $\times$ 1 m.

<!-- STEP -->
## The wheat bag in joules

To lift the 5 kg bag slowly, you push up with a force equal to its weight, $mg$. With $g = 10$ m/s$^2$, that is 5 $\times$ 10 = 50 N.

Lifting it by 1 m: $W$ = 50 N $\times$ 1 m = 50 J.

<!-- STEP -->
## Which force, on what

Always say which force does the work, and on which object. Here the force of your hands does work on the bag.

Your textbook calls the source of the force the agency.
