# Work as an Area

<!-- STEP -->
## A steady force

Draw a graph with force going up and displacement going across. A steady force of 10 N acting over 1 m gives a flat line, and a rectangle under it.

The area of the rectangle is 10 N $\times$ 1 m = 10 J. That area is the work.

<!-- STEP -->
## A changing force

Often the force changes as the object moves. The work is still the area under the line.

Split the area into simple shapes and add their areas.
