# Challenge: Load Lifter

<!-- STEP -->
## Hit the target exactly

A target work value is waiting at the top of the panel. Set the mass hanging on the hook, set how high you lift it, and choose the direction you pull from. Then press **Lift**.

- Overshoot the target and the run is wasted.
- Pull square across the lift and the readout says **zero**, every time. Zero never reaches a target.
- The only way onto the target is to make the force and the distance multiply out at exactly the right number.

<!-- STEP -->
## Read it before you pull

The sign is live as well. Lift upward against the weight and the work is positive. Set the mass down instead and the number goes negative - and the target will not take a negative value.

**There is no way to win this that does not go through $F \times s$.**
