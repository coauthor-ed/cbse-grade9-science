# Lifting Wheat Bags

<!-- STEP -->
## A force moves a bag

In science, **work is done when a force moves something.**

You lift a 5 kg wheat bag by 1 m. Your force points up. The bag moves 1 m up.

Displacement means how far something moved, and in which direction. Here the displacement is 1 m, upward.

<!-- STEP -->
## Three bags

Now lift three bags, one after the other, each by 1 m. You do three times the work.

A machine doing this job would need three times the fuel.
