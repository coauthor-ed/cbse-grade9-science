# Three Ways to Come Out With Nothing

<!-- STEP -->
## The definition, taken apart

$W = F \times s$ is a product, and a product has two factors. A product is zero whenever *either* factor is zero, which hands you two free tickets:

- Set the **force** to zero - nothing to multiply by.
- Set the **displacement** to zero - nothing to multiply.

Either dial at zero, and the readout drops to zero no matter what the other one says.

<!-- STEP -->
## The third ticket is not free

There is a third way to get nothing, and it is the one that catches people out. Neither factor is zero in it. Both are perfectly large.

It is not the *size* of the force and the *size* of the displacement that decide. It is which way they point.
