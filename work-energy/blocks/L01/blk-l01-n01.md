# Force Times Displacement

<!-- STEP -->
## Three times the force

Tie three bags together and lift them by 1 m. You need three times the force, and you do three times the work.

<!-- STEP -->
## Three times the displacement

Lift one bag to 3 m. The force is the same, but the displacement is three times as large. Again, three times the work.

<!-- STEP -->
## The rule

**Work done by a force = force $\times$ displacement in the direction of the force.**

$$W = F \times s$$

Here $W$ is the work, $F$ is the force and $s$ is the displacement. The rule is the same for a sideways push as for a lift.
