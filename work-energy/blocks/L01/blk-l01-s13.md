# Work Leaves Something Behind

<!-- STEP -->
## On the shelf

You lifted the 5 kg bag onto a shelf 1 m up. Your force did 50 J of work on it. Now nobody touches the bag.

<!-- STEP -->
## If it falls

If the bag falls, it can crush a paper cup below.

The work you did has given the bag something it did not have before. The next lesson gives it a name.
