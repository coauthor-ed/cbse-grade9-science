# No Displacement, No Work

<!-- STEP -->
## The wall did not move

The wall's displacement is 0. So $W = F \times 0 = 0$.

However hard you push, your force does no work on the wall.

<!-- STEP -->
## No force, no work

If there is no force, there is no work either. A puck sliding on smooth ice moves, but nothing pushes it along. $F = 0$, so $W = 0$.

<!-- STEP -->
## So why are you tired?

Your muscles keep tightening and relaxing as you push. That uses energy inside your body.

But it is not work done on the wall.
