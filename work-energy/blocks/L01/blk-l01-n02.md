# Lifting and Lowering a Dumbbell

<!-- STEP -->
## Lifting

A girl lifts a dumbbell. Her force points up and the dumbbell moves up.

Her force does positive work on the dumbbell.

<!-- STEP -->
## Lowering slowly

Now she lowers it slowly. Her force still points up, to stop it falling. But the dumbbell moves down.

Her force does negative work on the dumbbell.
