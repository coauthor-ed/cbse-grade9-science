# On the Moon

<!-- STEP -->
## The same throw

On the Moon, the pull of gravity is about one sixth of that on the Earth. An astronaut throws the same ball straight up, at the same speed, on the Earth and on the Moon.

Both times, the ball starts with the same kinetic energy.

<!-- STEP -->
## How high?

At the top, all that kinetic energy has become potential energy, $mgh$. On the Earth, the ball rises 8 m.

The mass and the energy are the same on the Moon, but $g$ is six times smaller. **So what must happen to $h$?**
