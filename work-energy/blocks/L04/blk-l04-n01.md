# Potential Energy

<!-- STEP -->
## The name

**Potential energy is energy stored in an object because its shape has changed, or in a system because of where its objects are.**

A stretched band, a squashed spring, two magnets pulled apart and a lifted ball all have potential energy.

<!-- STEP -->
## The ball's potential energy

The Earth is far more massive than the ball, so the Earth hardly moves towards it. So the energy stored by the ball and the Earth is simply called the ball's gravitational potential energy.

From now on, "potential energy" means this kind.
