# Energy Stored by Position

<!-- STEP -->
## Two magnets

Hold two magnets with unlike poles facing, and pull them apart. That takes a force. Let go, and they rush together and gain kinetic energy.

A small pea placed between two strong magnets can even be crushed. **The pair stored energy because of where the magnets were.**

<!-- STEP -->
## Two charges

A positive charge and a negative charge attract each other. Pull them apart, and they store energy too. Let them go, and they move together and can do work.

<!-- STEP -->
## A ball and the Earth

The Earth and a ball attract each other. Lift the ball and let go: the ball and the Earth rush towards each other.

The ball and the Earth together form a system. A system means objects that push or pull on each other, taken together. Lifted apart, this system stores energy.
