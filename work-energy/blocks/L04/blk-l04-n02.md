# Where U = mgh Works

<!-- STEP -->
## Near the Earth

$U = mgh$ works near the Earth's surface. Far from the Earth, $g$ becomes smaller, so this formula no longer gives the right value. You will meet the formula for far away in higher grades.

<!-- STEP -->
## Only height counts

For one object, $U$ changes only when its height changes. Moving sideways at the same height does not change $U$. Moving up does.

<!-- STEP -->
## Friction stores nothing

Drag a heavy box across a rough floor. You do work against friction, but that work is not stored: the box will not move back by itself.

The energy goes into heat and sound in the box and the floor.
