# U = mgh

<!-- STEP -->
## The work to lift

Lift an object of mass $m$ slowly from the ground to a height $h$. You push up with a force equal to its weight, $mg$. Here $g$ is the acceleration due to gravity, 10 m/s$^2$.

Work = force $\times$ displacement, so

$$W = mg \times h = mgh$$

<!-- STEP -->
## Stored as potential energy

That work is stored as potential energy, $U$. **$U = mgh$**, in joules (J). On the ground, we take $U = 0$.

A fielder throws a 200 g cricket ball about 10 m up. At the top, $U = 0.2 \times 10 \times 10 = 20$ J.
