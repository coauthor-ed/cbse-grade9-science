# Two Meters, and a Button Nobody Has Pressed

<!-- STEP -->
## Read the two meters

A ball is sitting on a shelf. Not moving. Two meters sit beside it, and the panel has both switched on.

- The **K meter** is at zero, and it will stay at zero, because the ball is not moving.
- The **U meter** is full, and it is full because of where the ball is.

Same ball. Same instant. One meter empty, one meter full.

**You now have two names for energy, and this ball can hold both at once.**

<!-- STEP -->
## The control in the corner

There is a third thing on that panel. A control, greyed out, labelled with the sign that means *added together*.

It is not the only one of its kind on the panel. There is one of those for a ball sitting on the ground, and one for a ball halfway down a fall, and the panel will let you put any of them side by side.

Nothing has pressed any of them yet. The panel is showing you the two meters separately on purpose.

**Somebody has to do the adding, and this lesson has not said what the sum is.**

<!-- STEP -->
## What happens when it is let go

So here is the situation the panel is holding still for you.

The ball is on a shelf with a full U meter and an empty K meter, and there is a button that would add the two numbers together. The button has not been pressed. And the ball has not been let go.

Once it is let go, the U meter will not stay full, and the K meter will not stay empty, and the ball will end on the floor with the two meters the other way round from how they started.

**What happens when it is let go - and does the total ever change?**
