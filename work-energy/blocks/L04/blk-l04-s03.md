# Energy Stored by Shape

<!-- STEP -->
## The band stores the work

Stretching the band took work. That work is stored in the band, because its shape has changed. When you let go, the band goes back to its shape and gives the energy to the stone.

A bent bow stores energy in the same way. **Work done to change an object's shape is stored in it.**

<!-- STEP -->
## A spring does the same

Push a ball against a spring and squash the spring against a wall. You do work on the spring, and it stores that energy while it stays squashed.

Let go, and the spring pushes the ball away. The stored energy becomes the ball's kinetic energy.

<!-- STEP -->
## Forces inside the spring

When you squash a spring, forces inside it push back, and you must push against them.

When you let go, these inside forces return the spring to its shape. They are the forces that do the work on the ball.
