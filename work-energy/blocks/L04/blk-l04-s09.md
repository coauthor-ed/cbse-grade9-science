# Lift or Stairs

<!-- STEP -->
## Two ways up

A student of mass 50 kg goes to the top of a building 72.5 m tall. One day the student goes up in the lift. Another day, the student climbs the stairs.

<!-- STEP -->
## The gain by lift

By lift, the student's potential energy rises by

$$mgh = 50 \times 10 \times 72.5 = 36\,250 \text{ J}$$

The stairs take a much longer path to the same top. **Does that change the gain?**
