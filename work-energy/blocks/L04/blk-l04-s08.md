# At the Top of the Throw

<!-- STEP -->
## At the top

Throw a ball straight up. It slows down as it rises. At the highest point it stops for an instant.

There its kinetic energy is zero, and its potential energy is the largest it gets.

<!-- STEP -->
## Still pulled down

At the top, the force on the ball is not zero. Gravity still pulls it down with its weight, $mg$. So its acceleration is still $g$, downwards.

**That is why the ball does not stay up, but falls back.**
