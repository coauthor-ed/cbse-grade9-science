# Drops into Sand

<!-- STEP -->
## A ball into sand

Drop a heavy ball into a tray of loose sand from a height of about 1 m. It makes a dent.

Now drop it from 2 m, at a different spot. This dent is deeper.

<!-- STEP -->
## Higher means more energy

Lifting the ball to 2 m took more work than lifting it to 1 m. So at 2 m the ball had more energy to push into the sand.

**The higher the ball is above the ground, the more potential energy it has.**
