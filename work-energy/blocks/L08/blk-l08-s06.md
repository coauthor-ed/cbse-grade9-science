# Small Force, Long Way

<!-- STEP -->
## The two ends move

Push the long end of a crowbar down a long way with a small force. The short end lifts the rock a little way with a large force.

<!-- STEP -->
## Work in, work out

Ignoring friction, the work you do at one end equals the work done on the rock at the other end:

**$F_1 \times d_1 = F_2 \times d_2$**

Here $F_1$ is your effort and $d_1$ how far your end moves; $F_2$ is the force on the rock and $d_2$ how far it rises. A lever reduces the force needed, not the work.
