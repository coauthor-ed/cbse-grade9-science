# Challenge: Lever Lab

<!-- STEP -->
## A load, a bar, and an effort you are stuck with

You have a target load to lift and a bar to lift it with. The effort you have available is **fixed** and you cannot ask for more.

What is yours to place is the bar: where the pivot sits along it, where the load hangs, and where the effort is applied.

Set it up and the bar will tell you the moment it is wrong.

<!-- STEP -->
## The bar only rests level when one thing is true

$$\text{effort} \times \text{effort arm} = \text{load} \times \text{load arm}$$

Slide any of the three and the bar tips. Move them until it does not, and the lift is yours.

**Every placement that balances is a win.** None of them is free: the work you put in is the work the load takes.
