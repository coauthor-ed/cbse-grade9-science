# A Ruler and a Pencil

<!-- STEP -->
## Try it

Lay a 30 cm scale across a pencil, with the pencil near one end. Put a stapler on that end. Now put one or two erasers on the far end.

The erasers lift the stapler.

<!-- STEP -->
## A lever

A light object has lifted a heavy one. The scale is a lever: a rigid bar that turns about a fixed point.
