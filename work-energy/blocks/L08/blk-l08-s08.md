# Mechanical Advantage of a Lever

<!-- STEP -->
## Arm over arm

From effort $\times$ effort arm = load $\times$ load arm:

$$MA = \frac{\text{load}}{\text{effort}} = \frac{\text{effort arm}}{\text{load arm}}$$

An effort arm of 60 cm and a load arm of 20 cm give $MA = 60 \div 20 = 3$. A longer effort arm gives a larger mechanical advantage.
