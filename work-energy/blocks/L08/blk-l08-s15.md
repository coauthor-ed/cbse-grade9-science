# Three Machines, Two Meters Each

<!-- STEP -->
## Side by side, at last

A pulley, a ramp and a lever, all three running at once on one panel. Each has an input meter and an output meter.

Run all three.

**None of them comes out ahead.** The output never beats the input.

<!-- STEP -->
## Three machines, one lesson

Look across the three rows:

- The **wheel** took a pull and gave back a load, and changed the direction.
- The **ramp** took a push and gave back a rise, trading force for distance.
- The **bar** took a push at one end and gave back a lift at the other, trading the same thing the other way round.

Three different-looking objects, one product, and not a single joule created by any of them.

<!-- STEP -->
## A gap on every meter

Look closely at each pair. The output reading is a little smaller than the input reading, every time.

**Where did the missing part go?** Hold on to that question. The next lesson answers it.
