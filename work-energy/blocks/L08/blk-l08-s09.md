# A Seesaw

<!-- STEP -->
## Five points

A seesaw has seats A and B on one side and D and E on the other. C is the fulcrum. AC = EC = 2 m, and BC = DC = 1 m.

<!-- STEP -->
## Where to sit

A child of mass 15 kg sits at A: $15 \times 2 = 30$. A child of mass 30 kg needs $30 \times d = 30$, so $d = 1$ m.

The second child should sit at seat D.
