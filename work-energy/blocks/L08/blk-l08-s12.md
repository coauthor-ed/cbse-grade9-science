# The Wheel That Should Never Stop

<!-- STEP -->
## Turn the handle

A crank wheel with a magnet on its rim. Turn it and it drives itself - the magnet pulls the next one round - and it seems it should keep going for ever.

There are two meters. One counts what goes in. One counts what comes out.

**Turn it a hundred times.**

<!-- STEP -->
## The gap, every single time

The output meter is a little behind the input meter. Not on one turn - on **every** turn, and the gap grows as the turning goes on.

There is a third meter. It is filling as fast as the gap grows, and it is labelled **heat**.

<!-- STEP -->
## Nobody has ever built one of these

There have been designs for this for as long as there have been machines: wheels and weights and magnets, each one supposed to run for ever without fuel.

**Not one has worked, and the reason is on the panel.** Friction at the pivot and resistance in the air take a little out on every turn, however good the bearings are.

**Ask where the difference between the two meters goes.** The next lesson answers it.
