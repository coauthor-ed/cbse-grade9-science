# A Spoon and Scissors

<!-- STEP -->
## Opening a lid

To open a tin lid, put the end of a spoon under the lid's edge. The rim of the tin is the fulcrum, the lid is the load and your hand gives the effort.

The long effort arm gives a large force on the lid.

<!-- STEP -->
## Cutting card

To cut something hard with scissors, put it near the pivot. The load arm is short, so you need less effort.
