# Three Classes of Lever

<!-- STEP -->
## What is in the middle

Class I: the fulcrum is in the middle, as in scissors, a crowbar and a seesaw.

Class II: the load is in the middle, as in a lemon squeezer, a wheelbarrow and a bottle opener.

Class III: the effort is in the middle, as in tweezers and a broom. Here the effort is larger than the load, but the load end moves further and faster.

<!-- STEP -->
## Tongs

Tongs appear in two classes. Tongs that cross at a pivot, like scissors, are class I. Tongs joined at one end, like tweezers, are class III.
