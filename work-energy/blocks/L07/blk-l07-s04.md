# A Fixed Pulley

<!-- STEP -->
## A wheel and a rope

A pulley is a wheel with a groove that holds a rope. A fixed pulley is fixed at the top. You pull the rope down, and the flag or the load goes up.

<!-- STEP -->
## Mechanical advantage 1

The effort equals the load, so the mechanical advantage is 1.

A fixed pulley changes only the direction of the force. But pulling down is easier than lifting up, because you can use your own weight.
