# Over a Step

<!-- STEP -->
## The ramp's length

A person uses a ramp to get an object over a step 30 cm high. The ramp covers 40 cm of floor. The step, the floor and the ramp make a right-angled triangle, so the ramp's length is 50 cm.

<!-- STEP -->
## Its mechanical advantage

$MA = \dfrac{L}{h} = \dfrac{50}{30} = 1.67$.
