# Challenge: Ramp Doctor

<!-- STEP -->
## Clear the ceiling, use the least

A load, a height that has to be reached, and a limit: **you may use no more than this much effort.**

That is the only constraint on your ramp. The height is fixed, the load is fixed, and the work is fixed whatever you do.

Lengthen the ramp and watch the effort reading fall. Stop at the first length that gets it under the ceiling.

**You are scored on the shortest ramp that keeps the effort at or under the ceiling.**

<!-- STEP -->
## Why the obvious answer is wrong

The obvious move is to make the ramp as long as the panel allows. That clears the ceiling easily, but it is the longest ramp, so it scores worst.

<!-- STEP -->
## What is actually being tested

The scene checks this equation every time you move anything:

$$F' \times L = mgh$$

The effort, the length and the height have to be the same division on every drag. Every ramp that keeps the effort under the ceiling is allowed; the one that scores best is the shortest of them.
