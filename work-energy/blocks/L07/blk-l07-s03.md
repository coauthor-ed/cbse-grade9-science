# Effort, Load, Mechanical Advantage

<!-- STEP -->
## Two forces

The force you apply to a machine is called the effort. The force the machine has to overcome is called the load.

<!-- STEP -->
## Mechanical advantage

**Mechanical advantage = load / effort.**

If a load of 600 N is moved with an effort of 200 N, the mechanical advantage is $600 \div 200 = 3$.
