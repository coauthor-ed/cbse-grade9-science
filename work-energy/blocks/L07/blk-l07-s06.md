# Measuring with a Spring Balance

<!-- STEP -->
## Lift it, then pull it

Lift a toy cart straight up with a spring balance. The balance reads the cart's weight.

Now pull the cart up a plank to the same height. The balance reads less.

<!-- STEP -->
## A gentler slope

Make the plank less steep, with the same height. The force is smaller again, but you pull the cart further.
