# A Box Too Heavy to Lift

<!-- STEP -->
## Straight up

You need to put a heavy box onto the back of a truck. To lift it straight up, you need a force equal to its weight.

<!-- STEP -->
## A simple machine

A plank from the ground to the truck makes the job easier. The plank is a simple machine.

A simple machine changes the size or the direction of a force. It does not reduce the total work.
