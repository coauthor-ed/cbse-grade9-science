# Movable Pulleys

<!-- STEP -->
## A pulley that moves

In a movable pulley, the load hangs from a pulley that moves up with it. One end of the rope is fixed at the top. You pull the other end.

<!-- STEP -->
## Half the effort

Two parts of the rope hold up the load, so the effort is about half the load. Ideally, the mechanical advantage is 2.

The trade: you pull 2 m of rope to lift the load by 1 m. Lifts and cranes use pulleys like these.
