# A Car Starts

<!-- STEP -->
## The engine's work

A car of mass 1000 kg starts from rest and reaches 72 km/h, which is 20 m/s, in 10 s. Ignore friction and air.

The engine's work becomes the car's kinetic energy: $\tfrac{1}{2} \times 1000 \times 20^2 = 200\,000$ J.

<!-- STEP -->
## The engine's power

Power = $200\,000 \text{ J} \div 10 \text{ s} = 20\,000$ W.
