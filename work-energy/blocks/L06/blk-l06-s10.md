# A Bag Nobody Can Lift

<!-- STEP -->
## The dial that does not help

A bag on the floor, and a mass dial next to it.

Push the dial up and the force needed to lift the bag climbs, and the panel shows the power that lifting it *would* need. Push it further and that power is enormous.

**The bag has not moved.** It is still on the floor, so at this moment no work is being done and no power is being developed.

<!-- STEP -->
## Being quick was never the problem

Everything in this lesson has been about the rate, and the rate could be changed with time, weight or height.

Not this time.

The panel can show a colossal power that would be needed, and no reading of it lifts the bag by a millimetre.

**Power is not the problem here. The size of the force is.**

<!-- STEP -->
## So what is the thing that actually moves it?

Somebody could come along and be strong. Or somebody could not, and there would still be a way - because the bag has not changed size, the floor has not changed height, and the job is exactly as big as before.

**The job is fixed. Only the way of doing it is still open.**

There is something that lets you do a job too big for you, without the job getting any smaller. It is the subject of the next lesson, and it does not make the bag lighter.
