# Challenge: Same Job, Your Pace

<!-- STEP -->
## Hit the target power

The job on the panel is fixed: one mass, lifted one height. The work cannot change.

What is yours to choose is **how long you take**.

Set your time and press **Lift**. The power reading follows, and the target is a number you have to land on exactly.

- Too slow and the power is under target.
- Too fast and the power is over.

**You cannot reach the target by changing the work. There is no dial for that.**

<!-- STEP -->
## Or hit the target time

The second round swaps the ends. The power is fixed, and the target is a **time**.

Your dials are the mass and the height. More work at the same power takes longer, since $t = W/P$:

- A heavier mass over the same height is more work, so it takes more time.
- A lower lift of the same mass is less work, so it takes less time.

Either route works. What counts is that the numbers agree.

<!-- STEP -->
## Work it out first

The power, the work and the time must obey $P = W/t$ on every run. A power that does not come from the work over the time is just a number you typed, and the panel will say so.
