# Higher, or Faster

<!-- STEP -->
## A crane

A crane lifts a load to the 10th floor of a building. Then it lifts the same load to the 20th floor, in double the time.

Twice the height needs twice the energy. Twice the energy in twice the time is the same power.

<!-- STEP -->
## A flag

A flag is raised up a pole using a pulley. The work depends on the flag's weight and the height of the pole.

Raise it twice as fast: the work is the same, the time is half, so the power is doubled.
