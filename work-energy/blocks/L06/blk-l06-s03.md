# Power

<!-- STEP -->
## The same work

Say you and your bag have a mass of 50 kg. Both climbs lift this mass by 3 m, so both do the same work: $mgh = 50 \times 10 \times 3 = 1500$ J.

The run does it faster.

<!-- STEP -->
## The rate of doing work

**Power is the rate of doing work:** $P = \dfrac{W}{t}$, where $W$ is the work done and $t$ is the time taken.

Run: $1500 \div 10 = 150$ W. Walk: $1500 \div 50 = 30$ W. The run's power is five times the walk's.

<!-- STEP -->
## The watt

The unit of power is the watt (W). 1 W is 1 J of work done each second: 1 W = 1 J/s.
