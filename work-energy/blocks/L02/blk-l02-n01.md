# Energy Is the Capacity to Do Work

<!-- STEP -->
## What makes things go

You walk to school. A ceiling fan turns. A car moves along the road.

Each of them needs energy. You get yours from food. The fan gets it from electricity. The car gets it from fuel.

<!-- STEP -->
## The meaning of energy

**Energy is the capacity to do work.**

Anything that can do work has energy. You can push, the fan can push air, and the car can push things out of its way.
