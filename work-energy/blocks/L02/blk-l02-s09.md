## Six of the seven get pushed aside

The board from four slides ago had seven panels on it. Now watch what happens to it.

The ledger you filled in on the work-energy slide can only ever see two things: work done, and energy changed. Light, sound, thermal, electrical, nuclear and chemical are all perfectly real - but we have no way yet to put a number on any of them, so on this board they step back.

One panel keeps its place, and it is the one holding all of the mechanical energy we have been talking about.

**Everything still standing on this board is energy due to where an object is, or how it is moving.**

<!-- STEP -->
## Those are the two columns

The board has been rebuilt with two, side by side.

- **MOTION** - a ball rolling across the grass, and a ball already in the air.
- **POSITION** - a ball sitting still on a shelf, with nothing touching it.

**A ball on that shelf is not doing anything, and its column is not empty.** That is the part that trips people up, so look at it before you move on.

Neither column has a number on it yet. The ledger can tell you when either of them changes, but it cannot tell you how big either one is.
