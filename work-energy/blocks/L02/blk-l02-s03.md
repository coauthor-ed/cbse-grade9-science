# Where the Work Came From

<!-- STEP -->
## Walking

Your legs are doing work on the road, and your muscles are doing work on your legs. Neither of those works came from nowhere.

It came from your last meal. The energy in food is stored in the chemical bonds that hold its molecules together, and a running body breaks those bonds and spends what was in them.

**Nothing in your leg is producing energy. It is spending a store that was filled earlier.**

<!-- STEP -->
## Turning a fan

A ceiling fan is driven by electricity, and electricity is not a fuel. It is a delivery system.

Something is burned, or dropped, or caught at a power station, and the energy in that something is pushed down a wire to wherever a switch is closed. The fan then spends it, spinning.

**A supply line does not create the energy it carries. It moves it.**

<!-- STEP -->
## Moving a car

Same shape again, one step further back. The fuel in the tank was not made in the engine.

It was refined from something that was living or buried long before the car existed, and the energy in it has been sitting in that store ever since. The engine is only the place where the handover happens.

Three quite different stores - dinner, a power station, a tank - and one thing in common:

**All of them are energy that was stored earlier, waiting to be spent.**
