# Work Gives Energy

<!-- STEP -->
## The throw

The fielder's force moves the ball forward, so the force does positive work on the ball.

The ball gains energy while the hand pushes it. Then it flies with that energy.

<!-- STEP -->
## The hit

The moving ball pushes the stumps and makes them move. The ball does work on the stumps.

Energy passes from the ball to the stumps. We say the energy is transferred.

<!-- STEP -->
## A flowerpot up high

A flowerpot lifted onto a balcony also gains energy, from the work done to raise it.

If it falls, it can damage what is below.
