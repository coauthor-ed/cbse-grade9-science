# Other Ways Energy Moves

<!-- STEP -->
## Heat

Work is one way to transfer energy. Heat is another. Energy flows from a hotter object to a colder object that it touches.

A spoon in hot tea gets warm this way.

<!-- STEP -->
## Without touching

Energy can also move without contact. The Sun's energy reaches the Earth by radiation, across empty space.

Energy also moves through electric circuits, and in sound waves.
