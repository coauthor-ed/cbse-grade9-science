# Challenge: Pay As You Go

<!-- STEP -->
## Set the push, pay for it, land on the speed

A cart sits on a smooth floor with a target energy pinned above it. Set how hard you push, set how many steps you push for, then press **Pay**.

The run goes one step at a time, and every step posts the same two entries:

- the **work** your push paid on that step
- the **energy** the cart is now carrying

Those two numbers have to be the same number. If they are not, the panel rejects the run and tells you so.

Land the cart exactly on the target energy. Hit it, and it counts. Push past it at any step and the run is wasted.

<!-- STEP -->
## The only way onto the target is through the ledger

A step's energy is not a separate dial - the panel fills that box from the work you paid on that step and from nowhere else. Any run in which the two boxes ever disagree fails on the spot, whatever the speed did.

**To win, the work you choose to pay must come out exactly equal to the energy the cart ends up holding.** No faster pressing, no luck, no pressing harder on the last step only.
