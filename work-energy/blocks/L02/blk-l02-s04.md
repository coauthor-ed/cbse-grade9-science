# The Work-Energy Theorem

<!-- STEP -->
## Work in, energy up

**Work done on an object = change in its energy.**

Push a cart with a steady force of 10 N over 2 m on a smooth floor. You do 10 N $\times$ 2 m = 20 J of work on it. Its energy rises by 20 J.

<!-- STEP -->
## Other cases

This also holds when the force changes as it pushes, and for a group of objects together. A group of objects we study together is called a system.

Energy is measured in joules, like work.
