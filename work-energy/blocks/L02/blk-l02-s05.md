# A Carrom Shot

<!-- STEP -->
## Striker on coin

The moving striker hits a white coin at rest. The striker pushes the coin the way the coin moves. So the striker does positive work on the coin, and the coin's energy rises.

<!-- STEP -->
## Coin on striker

The coin pushes back on the striker, against the striker's motion. So the coin does negative work on the striker, and the striker's energy falls.

<!-- STEP -->
## Coin on coin

The white coin then hits a black coin, and the same thing happens again. The black coin gains energy and slides into the pocket.
