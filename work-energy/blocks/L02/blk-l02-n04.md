# Pedalling a Bicycle

<!-- STEP -->
## Where the energy goes

You pedal along a flat road. The energy from your muscles goes into the bicycle's motion. It also goes into heat, in your body, the tyres and the chain, and into a little sound.

<!-- STEP -->
## At a steady speed

Once you ride at a steady speed, the bicycle's kinetic energy stops growing.

Everything your muscles still give becomes heat and sound.
