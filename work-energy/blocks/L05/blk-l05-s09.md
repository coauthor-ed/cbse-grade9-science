# An Escape Ramp

<!-- STEP -->
## A truck without brakes

A truck of mass 10000 kg moves at 72 km/h, which is 20 m/s, when its brakes fail. The driver takes it up a sand ramp. The ramp is at 30°: it rises 1 m for every 2 m along it. The sand pushes back on the truck with a force of 50000 N.

<!-- STEP -->
## Where the energy goes

Its kinetic energy is $K = \tfrac{1}{2} \times 10000 \times 20^2 = 2\,000\,000$ J.

After it moves a length $d$ up the ramp, it has risen $\tfrac{d}{2}$, so it gains $U = 10000 \times 10 \times \tfrac{d}{2} = 50000 \times d$. The sand does work $-50000 \times d$, which becomes heat.

<!-- STEP -->
## How long a ramp

The truck stops when all its kinetic energy is used: $2\,000\,000 = 50000d + 50000d = 100000d$.

So $d = 20$ m. The ramp must be at least 20 m long.
