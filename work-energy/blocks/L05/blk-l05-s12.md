# Challenge: Set the Crater

<!-- STEP -->
## Hit the target depth

There is a patch of wet sand with a target depth marked on it, and a coconut to drop into it.

Two dials: **how high you drop from**, and **how heavy the coconut is**. Press **Drop** and the whole chain runs - the fall, the speed at the sand, the work done against the sand's resistance, and the crater that comes out of it.

You do not set a depth. You set a drop, and the depth is whatever that drop earns.

<!-- STEP -->
## There are two ways to miss

**Too little.** The crater is shallow and the target is not met, and no amount of retrying at that setting will help - the energy is too small.

**Too much.** The crater is deeper than the target.

The energy is $mgh$, so a heavier coconut from a lower height can dig as deep as a lighter one from higher. **Height and mass trade off**, and you can compare two drops that reach the same depth.

<!-- STEP -->
## What is actually being tested

The crater depth is not computed from your dials directly. It is worked out by the same chain the last slide laid out, in this order:

1. the energy the coconut carries down,
2. the speed that energy gives at the sand,
3. the work that speed does against the resistance,
4. the depth that work digs.

The scene keeps the energy on screen the whole time so you can see which step let you down.
