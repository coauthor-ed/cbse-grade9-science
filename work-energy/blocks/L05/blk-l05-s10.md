# Lower Each Time

<!-- STEP -->
## A ball on a track

A ball rolls down a track in a science park, starting from a tower. It climbs hill A, then C, then D, then E. Each hill it reaches is lower than the one before.

<!-- STEP -->
## Why lower

Friction and air take a little energy at every stretch, as heat. With less mechanical energy left, the ball cannot climb as high.

The total energy, including the heat, stays the same.
