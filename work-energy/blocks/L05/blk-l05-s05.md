# A Pendulum

<!-- STEP -->
## P, Q and R

Tie a small ball (the bob) to a string, pull it to one side to P, and let go.

At P the bob is at rest, with only potential energy. At Q, the lowest point, it has only kinetic energy. At R, the other end, it stops again, with only potential energy.

<!-- STEP -->
## A real pendulum stops

R is almost as high as P. Each swing is a little smaller, and a real pendulum slowly stops.

Friction at the support and air resistance take a little energy away at every swing, as heat.
