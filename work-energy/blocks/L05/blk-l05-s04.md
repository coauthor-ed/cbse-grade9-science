# A Falling Ball, Step by Step

<!-- STEP -->
## After a time t

After falling for a time $t$, the ball's speed is $gt$, and it has fallen $\tfrac{1}{2}gt^2$.

Its kinetic energy is $\tfrac{1}{2}m(gt)^2 = \tfrac{1}{2}mg^2t^2$.

Its potential energy has fallen by $mg \times \tfrac{1}{2}gt^2 = \tfrac{1}{2}mg^2t^2$.

<!-- STEP -->
## The same amount

The kinetic energy gained equals the potential energy lost. This is the **conservation of mechanical energy**.

It holds only when no other force, such as friction or air resistance, acts.
