# Halfway, Half

<!-- STEP -->
## Halfway down

Halfway down, the ball's height is $\tfrac{h}{2}$, so its potential energy is $\tfrac{1}{2}mgh$. The potential energy it lost, the other half, has become kinetic energy.

<!-- STEP -->
## At the ground

Just before it lands, $U = 0$ and $K = mgh$.

**The total stays $mgh$ all the way down.**
