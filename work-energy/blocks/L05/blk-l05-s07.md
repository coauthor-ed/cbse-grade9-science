# Answering the Three Questions

<!-- STEP -->
## Heavy or light

The speed at the bottom depends only on the height. A heavy child and a light child on the same slide reach the same speed.

<!-- STEP -->
## Three slides

All three playground slides start at the same platform and end on the ground. Without friction, all three give the same speed at the bottom.

<!-- STEP -->
## Real slides

Real slides have friction. A longer, rougher path turns more energy into heat, so the child arrives a little slower.
