# Down a Slide

<!-- STEP -->
## Height becomes speed

A child slides down a slide of height $h$. Ignore friction. All the potential energy $mgh$ becomes kinetic energy $\tfrac{1}{2}mv^2$ at the bottom.

<!-- STEP -->
## The speed at the bottom

$mgh = \tfrac{1}{2}mv^2$, so $v = \sqrt{2gh}$.

The mass cancels, and the shape of the slide does not appear. A slide 1.8 m high gives $v = \sqrt{2 \times 10 \times 1.8} = 6$ m/s.
