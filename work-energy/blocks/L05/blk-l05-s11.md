# A Coconut Falls

<!-- STEP -->
## The fall

A coconut of mass 1.5 kg falls from a tree 10 m high onto wet sand. Its potential energy at the top is $mgh = 1.5 \times 10 \times 10 = 150$ J.

Just before it lands, $v = \sqrt{2 \times 10 \times 10}$, about 14.1 m/s.

<!-- STEP -->
## The dent

The sand pushes back with a force of 3000 N and takes all 150 J (ignore the tiny extra fall into the sand). So 150 J = 3000 N $\times$ depth.

The depth of the dent is 0.05 m, or 5 cm.
