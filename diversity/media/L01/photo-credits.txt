blk-l01-s04.json attaches these seven photos (the three above plus
great-hornbill.jpg/rufous-necked-hornbill.jpg, already credited earlier in
this file, and oriental-pied-hornbill.jpg/wreathed-hornbill.jpg, credited
above) directly as `photoDiagram` nodes (docs/photo-diagrams.md,
PhotoDiagram.js) - rendered live by the engine, not pre-composited into a
flat image. An earlier draft of this slide baked two of these into static
JPEGs (ecology-web.jpg/hornbill-record.jpg via Python/Pillow); both are
superseded and deleted now that the engine renders the same relationship
live, with per-photo alt text and real theme support neither baked image
had.

All are original public downloads of openly licensed photographs (no textbook artwork extracted), resized/re-compressed for PWA delivery (longest edge capped ~1400px, JPEG quality 82 - originals were up to 14MB; animal/flower/insect thumbnails above are additionally centre-cropped to square 360x360). Record kept per docs/copyright-and-sourcing.md; a human reviewer should verify license pages before human-approval.
