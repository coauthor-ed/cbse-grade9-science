## Two Varieties, One Dry Spell

Why can some plants survive long droughts while others cannot? Even one crop has many varieties. Here are two made-up ones: A wilts early in a dry spell, and B lasts longer. One field grows only A, and the other grows both.

## Spreading the Risk

The mixed field keeps some plants standing, though it still loses some. For centuries, farmers have used their practical knowledge to keep diverse crop varieties, with useful characteristics such as drought tolerance, pest resistance and the ability to grow in poor soil. They saw that diversity reduces the risk of crop failure.

So biodiversity is more than a list of organisms. It also covers the varieties within one crop and the variety of habitats.

## How Classification Helps a Farmer

Some insects protect crops, and others damage them. To tell which is which, a farmer must be able to group and name them.
