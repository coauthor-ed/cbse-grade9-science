## Four Hornbills in Pakke

The Pakke Tiger Reserve in Arunachal Pradesh is home to four species of hornbill: the Rufous-necked, Oriental Pied, Great and Wreathed Hornbill. These large birds nest only in large, old trees with suitable cavities, and they feed on specific fruits.
