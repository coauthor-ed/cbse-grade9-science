## Present Is Not the Same as Safe

A list shows who is present, not what they depend on. Hornbills raise their young in cavities in old trees, so losing those trees cuts the next generation even while the adults are still being seen.

<!-- STEP -->
## Plants Give Food and Oxygen

Each species, large or small, plays an important role in nature, and this forest shows how. Start with the tree: plants produce the food and oxygen that other organisms use.

<!-- STEP -->
## Animals Pollinate and Spread Seeds

Bees carry pollen between flowers, so the tree can form fruit. A hornbill eats the fruit and drops the seed in another part of the forest, where a new tree may grow.

<!-- STEP -->
## Microorganisms Recycle Nutrients

Fungi and bacteria break down fallen leaves and waste. The nutrients return to the soil, and the roots take them up again.

## Four Pressures on the Forest

Human activities are reducing biodiversity through deforestation, pollution, the overuse of resources and climate change. Tap each one to see what it touches in this forest.

**When one species disappears, others that depend on it may also decline and, in time, disappear.**
