## The Dancing Deer

The **Sangai**, the dancing deer, is endemic to Manipur. It was declared extinct in 1951, and in 1953 naturalists found it again. This photo was taken at the Manipur Zoological Garden.

<!-- STEP id="phumdi" -->
## A Floor That Floats

In the wild the deer spends much of its time on **phumdis**, floating beds of soil with rooted plants and rich organic matter. They lie on Loktak Lake in Keibul Lamjao National Park (your book prints Lamjo), and they vary from a few centimetres to two metres thick.

<!-- STEP id="protection" -->
## On the List, but Not Yet Safe

The phumdis are now degenerating, and the Sangai is listed in the IUCN Red Data list. Being on that list shows the danger, but it does not mend the habitat.

**Habitat loss will significantly affect the Sangai, so protection must reach the phumdi as well as the deer.**
