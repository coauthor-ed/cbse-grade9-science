## What If a Forest Guard Could Only Report One Fact?

Imagine Pakke's forest guard could only write down one single fact each year: either "hornbills present" or "hornbills absent" - nothing else at all.

Working through what that would actually mean:

- The record would keep reading "present" for all four hornbill species right up until the very last bird was gone - it would never show that their nesting trees are disappearing along the way.
- Catching the real problem needs tracking nesting-tree availability and fruiting-tree health specifically, not just whether the birds themselves are still being seen each year.
- This is the exact same failure the whole course opened with in lesson one: a coarse yes/no record can stay unchanged right up until a real loss becomes irreversible.

A single present/absent fact is exactly the kind of record this course has spent eleven lessons showing you how to outgrow.
