## More Than Segments

Both animals do have segments, so that first look was correct. A closer look finds more: the beetle has jointed legs and a hard outer covering, and the earthworm has neither.

<!-- STEP -->
## Two Phyla

The earthworm belongs to **Annelida**, the segmented worms. The beetle belongs to **Arthropoda**, with insects, crabs and spiders.

Arthropods have jointed legs with a hard outer skeleton. A tiger has jointed legs too, but its skeleton is inside. **A group is decided by a set of features, not by one feature two animals share.**
