## A Wave Along the Body

An earthworm has no legs. Its muscles make each part of the body either long and thin or short and thick.

Press **Crawl** and follow the wave. It runs from the front of the body towards the back, while the worm moves forward.

<!-- STEP -->
## Why Segments Help

A nerve cord controls and coordinates the muscles. Because the body is divided into segments, each part can change shape in turn, and this gives greater flexibility and more precise control of movement.
