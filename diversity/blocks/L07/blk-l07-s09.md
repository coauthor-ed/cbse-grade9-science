## Notochord, Then Backbone

**Chordates** have a notochord at least once during life. Protochordates like Amphioxus have no backbone, while vertebrates have a vertebral column, or backbone.

In some chordates the notochord comes first and the backbone forms in its place. The notochord itself does not turn into bone.

## Three Separate Questions

Hard parts inside? Notochord? Backbone? A starfish, Amphioxus and a fish answer differently, so **each question has to be asked on its own.**

Your book uses non-chordata and invertebrates for one group. Strictly, invertebrate means no backbone, so the word also fits protochordates.
