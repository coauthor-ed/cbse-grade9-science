## Hard Parts on the Inside

You might expect a skeleton inside the body to mean a backbone. Starfish and sea urchins show otherwise.

They belong to **Echinodermata**, a name made of *echinos*, spiny, and *derma*, skin. Their hard internal skeleton is made of calcium carbonate, and it gives protection and controlled movement.

## Plates Are Not a Rod

A **notochord** is one particular structure: a flexible rod along the body. A starfish has hard plates and no such rod, so echinoderms stay with the non-chordata.

**Hard parts inside the body do not mean a notochord.**
