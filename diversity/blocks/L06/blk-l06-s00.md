## What Makes an Animal?

Animals are multicellular and heterotrophic: each body has many cells, and it depends on other organisms for food.

Most animals can also move from place to place, respond quickly and act in a coordinated way. These abilities help them search for food and avoid predators.

<!-- STEP -->
## One Rod Splits the Kingdom

One major way to classify animals is to ask about the **notochord**, a flexible rod-shaped structure.

Animals without a notochord are the non-chordata, also called invertebrates. Animals with a notochord are the chordata.

## Eight Phyla Ahead

The non-chordata are divided into eight large groups, and each group is called a phylum (plural: phyla).

We start with the first four, and one question connects them: how does this body get its food in?
