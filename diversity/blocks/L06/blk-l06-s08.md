## Two Long Bodies

A roundworm and an earthworm can look alike, since both are long and tube-shaped. Look closer, though: the roundworm's body is smooth, and the earthworm's is divided into repeated segments.

An earthworm belongs to a fifth phylum.

## Four Branches on the Tree

Four phyla now sit on the tree, and each gets food in by a different plan: water through pores, one opening with tissues, one opening with organs, and a route with two openings.

Next we ask how bodies are supported and moved, and the segments are where that begins.
