## Tentacles Catch the Prey

Hydra, jellyfish and corals belong to the phylum **Cnidaria**. A sponge depends on water currents to bring food through its pores, but a cnidarian catches its prey with tentacles.

Press **Feed** to watch Hydra catch one.

## Cells Working as Tissues

A **tissue** is a group of cells that work together on one job. Cnidarians have tissues, so specialised cells can do specific work, and the tentacles are one example.

This is a major change from the sponge, whose cells have no such organisation.

## One Opening, Two Jobs

Food goes in through a single opening. When digestion is over, the waste comes back out through the same opening.

**One opening does both jobs.** Your book asks what limits this body plan. Here is one idea to test: food and waste share one route, so they cannot both move along it at the same time.
