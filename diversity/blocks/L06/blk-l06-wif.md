## What If the Water Stopped Moving Through a Sponge?

A sponge's cells don't chase food or coordinate the way an organ would - they rely on water flowing through internal channels to bring food and oxygen to them.

Working through what that would actually mean:

- A sponge cell sitting deep inside the body, far from any opening, would starve. Sponge cells don't chase food themselves - without water carrying food and oxygen to them, nothing else would deliver it.
- "Many cells, each doing its own specialised job" would not be enough on its own - it's the moving water that actually connects those cells to a food supply in the first place.
- This is exactly why a sponge has channels running through its whole body, not just pores on the surface: those channels carry the moving water deep inside, doing the delivery job a mouth or legs would do in another animal.

A sponge's entire feeding plan depends on that moving water - take it away, and cellular organisation alone can't replace it.
