You have now placed living things in Monera, Protista and Fungi. Each kingdom holds its own surprises: bacteria that make food, a protist that does it only in light, and a fungus with one cell.

## From Water to Land

A cyanobacterium takes what it needs straight from the water around it. No part of it is far from that water. The leaves of a tall plant are far from the water at its roots.

The next lesson follows the plants, and asks how they moved from water to land.
