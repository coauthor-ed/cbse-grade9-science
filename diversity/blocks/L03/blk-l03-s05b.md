## Your Turn: A Pond in a Bottle

You can see protists for yourself in the school laboratory, with your teacher present. Make a hay infusion:

1. Collect mowed grass, straw or fodder.
2. Fill one fourth of a small glass bottle with it.
3. Fill the bottle with stagnant water or pond water, and mix.
4. Cover it with muslin cloth, tied with a thread.
5. Keep it aside, undisturbed, for a week.

<!-- STEP -->
## Look at One Drop

Open the cloth just enough to put a dropper in, and carefully take a drop. Put it on a clean slide and observe it under a microscope.

Do you see anything moving? Compare what you see with the Amoeba, Paramecium and Euglena you just met.

<!-- STEP -->
## Stay Safe

The infusion may smell bad, so take these precautions:

- Wear a lab coat, a mask and hand gloves.
- Do not throw the infusion away yourself. Your school discards it after autoclaving, which means heating it under steam pressure to kill what is growing.
