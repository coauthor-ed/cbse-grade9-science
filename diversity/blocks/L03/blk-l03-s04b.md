## Oxygen From Tiny Cells

Cyanobacteria were among the first organisms to produce oxygen through photosynthesis. About 2.5 billion years ago, oxygen collected in the atmosphere, and that made the Earth suitable for other forms of life.

## Layers in Old Rock

Fossils of ancient cyanobacteria are found in structures called stromatolites. They have been found in Rajasthan and Madhya Pradesh.

**Some of the earliest evidence of life on the Earth is the work of these one-celled prokaryotes.**
