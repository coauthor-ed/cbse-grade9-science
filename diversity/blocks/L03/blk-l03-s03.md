## Yeast Belongs in Fungi

"One cell with a nucleus means Protista" is a sensible rule, and yeast is where it fails. Fungi are mostly multicellular, but not always. Yeast has a chitin wall and it absorbs its food, as a mushroom does, so it belongs in Fungi.

**The number of cells alone does not decide a kingdom.**

## The Wall Parts Them

Amoeba has no cell wall at all. Protista holds single-celled eukaryotes with no cell wall, or with a wall made of cellulose. So the wall check sends Amoeba one way and yeast the other.

## The Cell With No Nucleus

A bacterium is one cell too, but its genetic material is not closed inside a nucleus. That makes it a prokaryote, so it goes to Monera before the wall check even starts.
