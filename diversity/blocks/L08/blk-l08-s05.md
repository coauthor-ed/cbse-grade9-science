## Both Lay Eggs

A lizard and a sparrow both lay eggs and both breathe with lungs, so these two features cannot separate them. Their body covering can: a lizard has dry scales, and a sparrow has feathers.

<!-- STEP -->
## Two Kinds of Eggshell

Many reptiles lay eggs with a soft, leathery shell, and birds lay eggs with a hard shell. These are broad patterns, since not every reptile lays eggs.

<!-- STEP -->
## A Bird That Cannot Fly

An ostrich cannot fly, yet it has feathers and lays hard-shelled eggs, so it is a bird. **Feathers mark a bird. Flying does not.**
