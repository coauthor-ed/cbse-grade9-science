## Five Names, Five Clues

Each of the five groups also has a name from Latin or Greek: Pisces, Amphibia, Reptilia, Aves and Mammalia. They look strange at first, but most of them are plain words.

<!-- STEP -->
## Read the Pieces

Read the pieces of each name, then pick the animal it describes. The name Amphibia holds the whole story of a frog, as you will see soon.
