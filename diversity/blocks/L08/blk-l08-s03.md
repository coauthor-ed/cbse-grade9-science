## Milk for the Young

The rule fails. A dolphin mother has **mammary glands**, which make milk, and she feeds her calf on it. A tigress feeds her cubs in the same way, and living in the sea does not change this.

## Lungs, Not Gills

A dolphin breathes air with lungs, so it must come up to the surface. It has no gills. Its body suits swimming very well, but that does not make it a fish: the dolphin is a mammal.

## Five Groups

Vertebrates are classified into five groups: fish, amphibians, reptiles, birds and mammals. We sort them by the broad patterns of their habitat use, body covering and reproduction, so where an animal lives is only one part of the picture.
