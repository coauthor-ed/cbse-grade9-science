## Fins and Gills

A fish does two separate jobs in water. Its fins help it move, steer and balance. Its gills take in the oxygen that is dissolved in the water, so a fin is not a breathing organ.

## A Frog's Two Lives

A frog's eggs develop in water. The tadpole that hatches breathes with gills, as a fish does. As it grows, legs appear and the tail goes. The adult moves on land and in water, and breathes with lungs and through its moist skin.

## Why a Frog Is an Amphibian

A young stage in water with gills, then an adult with lungs and moist skin: this pattern marks **Amphibia**. A tadpole lives like a fish for a while, but we place an animal by its whole life. Many amphibians follow this pattern, though not every one does.
