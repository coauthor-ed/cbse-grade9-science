## New to Science, Not New to Earth

The frog did not appear in 2003: that year only tells us when people described it. Fossils show that it belongs to an ancient family of frogs, so the date of a discovery and the age of a family are two separate facts.

## What a Fossil Is

**Fossils** are preserved remains of plants and animals, found in layers of rocks, sand and mud. They help us understand how life has changed over millions of years.

<!-- STEP -->
## Reading the Layers

Layers form one on top of another, so in undisturbed rock a lower layer is older. Generally, older layers contain simpler organisms, while newer layers show more complex forms.

Tap the layers to dig down. Simpler does not mean worse: simple organisms still live all around us.

## Fossils in India

From giant dinosaurs to early humans and ancient plants, important fossils have been found in India.

**Birbal Sahni** studied fossil plants and founded the Birbal Sahni Institute of Palaeosciences (BSIP) in Lucknow. His work helped link present-day plants with their ancestors, and showed that life on Earth has a long, connected history.
