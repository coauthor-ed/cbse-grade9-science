## The Same Frog, a Better Explanation

Classification has changed many times, and the purple frog did not change with it. What changes is our explanation of how living things are related.

The discovery of this frog helped scientists understand ancient amphibian groups. It also showed the need to protect biodiversity in the Western Ghats.
