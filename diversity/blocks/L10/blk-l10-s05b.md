## One Drop of Pond Water

You might think that a change in classification means scientists were wrong. Look at one drop of pond water to see why that is too simple.

To the naked eye the drop is almost clear. A system built on what the eye can see, like Aristotle’s, sorted plants and animals, and it worked for its time.

<!-- STEP -->
## New Tools, New Organisms

Microscopes and staining techniques showed microorganisms in that same drop. The drop had not changed, but people could now see more, so the groups had to change.

<!-- STEP -->
## Why DNA Goes Deeper

DNA carries the instructions for a cell’s growth and function, so comparing DNA shows more than an outline can. It can separate two microbes that look the same.

**When classification changes, science is working: we reason again with better evidence.**
