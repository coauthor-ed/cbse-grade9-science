## Genetic Material, but No Cell

You met viruses in earlier classes. A virus contains genetic material, as living things do, but it lacks cellular organisation: it has no cell membrane, no cytoplasm and no nucleus.

<!-- STEP -->
## Inactive Outside a Host

Outside a host cell, a virus stays inactive. Inside one, the host cell itself makes new copies of the virus. Press **Enter a cell** to watch this happen.

<!-- STEP -->
## Outside the Five Kingdoms

Every kingdom holds organisms made of cells, and the very first test asks about the cell. A virus has no cell, so it cannot enter the system at all.

This shows a limit of classification: a system can sort only what its criteria can describe.
