## A Frog That Is Easy to Miss

Meet the purple frog, *Nasikabatrachus sahyadrensis*, from Kerala. Its species name comes from the Sahyadri Hills.

It lives underground for most of the year and comes out only during the monsoon to breed, so few people ever see it.

<!-- STEP -->
## Described in 2003

Scientists described this frog in 2003. The photo shows its rounded body, pointed snout and short limbs, but a photo cannot show how old its family is.
