## Comparing DNA

Whittaker’s five kingdoms grouped organisms better than earlier systems, but they could not fully explain the diversity of life. Two microbes can look alike and still differ deep inside.

Every living cell contains **DNA**, which carries the instructions for its growth and function. Organisms with similar DNA are considered to have a common ancestry. Press **Compare DNA** to line up these two.

## Three Domains

Based on genetic data, **Carl Woese** proposed the **three domain system** in 1977. The three domains are Bacteria, Archaea and Eukarya, and each is broader than a kingdom.

<!-- STEP -->
## Where the Kingdoms Went

Monera’s members split into two domains, Bacteria and Archaea. Every organism with a nucleus goes into the third, Eukarya. Tap a domain to see what it holds.

This system showed that microscopic life is far more diverse than people had believed. The five kingdoms remain a simple and useful scheme for school.
