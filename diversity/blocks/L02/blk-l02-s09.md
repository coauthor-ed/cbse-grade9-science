## A Sixth Organism

Your key handled five organisms. Now bring in **yeast**, the tiny organism that makes bhatura dough rise. We know two things about it: it is one cell, and it has a nucleus.

Send it down this key: one cell, with a nucleus. The key says Protista.

## Two Gaps

This key was only ever tested on Amoeba, so "one cell with a nucleus means Protista" was never a safe rule. We still do not know yeast's cell wall or how it gets its food.

**A key that works on five organisms can still fail on the sixth.**
