## A Third Kingdom

Amoeba and Paramecium move like animals, but each one is a single cell, while plants and animals are multicellular. So in 1866 Ernst Haeckel added a third kingdom, **Protista**, for unicellular organisms.

<!-- STEP -->
## A Fourth Kingdom

As microscopes improved, scientists noticed an important difference. An Amoeba has a true nucleus, and a bacterium does not. Both are single cells, yet they differ deeply, so bacteria were placed in a separate kingdom, **Monera**.

## Not Just Germs

You may think all bacteria are germs. Some bacteria do cause diseases, but many are useful, like the *Lactobacillus* that helps turn milk into curd.

**Level of organisation and cell type are two separate tests.**

The mushroom still has no home, though, because it is neither one cell nor a plant.
