## What If There Were Still Only Two Kingdoms?

Imagine biologists had stopped at just two kingdoms - Plantae and Animalia - and never added Monera, Protista or Fungi at all.

Working through what that would actually mean:

- A mushroom would have nowhere honest to go. It neither makes its own food like a plant nor ingests food the way an animal does - it would have to be forced into one of the two boxes, hiding the very absorb-don't-make-or-ingest evidence that later earned Fungi its own kingdom.
- A bacterium would have to be squeezed into whichever box happened to hold nucleus-bearing organisms, even though it has no nucleus at all - erasing the one difference that later separated Monera from everything else.
- Curd-making Lactobacillus, a biogas bacterium, and a fully-formed green plant would all count as "basically the same kind of organism" by default - even though one is a single cell with no nucleus and the other is many-celled and photosynthesises.

Two kingdoms couldn't hold any of these without hiding a real difference. That's exactly why this lesson kept adding kingdoms - each new one fixed one specific organism the older system couldn't honestly place.
