## One Cell, or Many?

Euglena fits both halves of the rule, so it has no clear place, and movement cannot be the test. Scientists needed tests that give one answer.

The first is **level of organisation**. A **unicellular** organism is one cell. A **multicellular** organism is many cells working together.

<!-- STEP -->
## Is There a Nucleus?

The second test is **cell type**. A **eukaryote** has a nucleus with a membrane around it. A **prokaryote** has no such membrane, so its genetic material lies loose in the cell.

Size does not settle this. You have to look for the membrane.

<!-- STEP -->
## Where Does the Food Come From?

The third test is **mode of nutrition**. **Autotrophic** organisms make their own food by photosynthesis. **Heterotrophic** organisms depend on others for food.

Euglena, in sunlight, is autotrophic. Amoeba takes in food, so it is heterotrophic. Both of them move, which shows that movement and food are separate questions.
