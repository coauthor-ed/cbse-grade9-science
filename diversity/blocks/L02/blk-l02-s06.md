## First Test: Cell Type

Here is the five kingdom system as a tree, one test at a time. Prokaryotes go to **Monera**. Eukaryotes go up the trunk for more tests.

<!-- STEP -->
## Second Test: Level of Organisation

Among eukaryotes, unicellular organisms such as Amoeba and Euglena go to **Protista**. A protist may or may not have a cell wall. The multicellular ones go on.

<!-- STEP -->
## Third Test: Cell Structure

Multicellular organisms with no cell wall, which depend on others for food, form **Animalia**. Those with a cell wall go on to one last test.

<!-- STEP -->
## Fourth Test: Mode of Nutrition

Walls of chitin belong to heterotrophic decomposers, **Fungi**. Walls of cellulose belong to autotrophic producers, **Plantae**.

These four criteria are the basis of the five kingdom classification: cell type, level of organisation, cell structure and mode of nutrition.
