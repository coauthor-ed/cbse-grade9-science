## Three Ways to Get Food

A mushroom is not a plant that lost its green colour. It is a **fungus**, and it feeds in its own way:

- A green plant makes its own food by photosynthesis.
- A fungus breaks food down outside its body, then absorbs the nutrients.
- An animal takes food into its body and digests it inside.

<!-- STEP -->
## The Cell Wall

**Cell structure** gives another test. Plant cells have a wall made of cellulose, and fungal cells have a wall made of chitin. Animal cells have no cell wall.

<!-- STEP -->
## A Home for the Mushroom

A chitin wall and food absorbed from outside: that is Kingdom **Fungi**. With this fifth kingdom, the system holds Monera, Protista, Fungi, Plantae and Animalia.

**Use cell and food evidence together, never movement alone.**
