## Two Patterns of Veins

Flowering plants fall into two groups, monocots and dicots, and a leaf gives a clue. Monocots such as grass usually have parallel veins. Dicots such as beans usually have veins in a net.

<!-- STEP -->
## Inside the Seed

A cotyledon is a seed leaf, already part of the embryo. A monocot has one and a dicot has two. Vein pattern is a clue with exceptions, so the seed gives the firmer answer.

## A Book of Indian Plants

One of the earliest scientific books on Indian plants, Hortus Malabaricus, was compiled in the 17th century by Hendrik van Rheede. He had the help of Itty Achudan, an Indian herbalist, botanist and physician, and of other local experts.

It describes hundreds of plant species and their medicinal uses, which shows traditional knowledge and science working together.
