## Five Groups, One Board

Each plant group solves the problems of survival in its own way. Start with the body: does it have true roots, stems and leaves?

<!-- STEP -->
## Who Has Transport Tissue?

Xylem and phloem carry water and food through the body. Pteridophytes, gymnosperms and angiosperms have them. Thallophytes and bryophytes do not.

<!-- STEP -->
## Who Reproduces Without Water?

Thallophytes, bryophytes and pteridophytes need water to reproduce. Gymnosperms and angiosperms do not need it for fertilisation.

<!-- STEP -->
## Who Makes Seeds and Fruits?

Only gymnosperms and angiosperms make seeds, and only angiosperms enclose them in fruits.

**Early plants relied on water for support and reproduction. Gradually, plant groups evolved transport tissues, seeds and flowers.**
