## A Seed Is a Package

"Naked" sounds like "unprotected", but it says only that no fruit is around the seed. The seed itself protects the embryo, the young plant inside, and it holds stored food.

**A naked seed lacks a fruit. It does not lack protection or food.**

## Leaves for Dry Air

Gymnosperms have needle-like or scale-like leaves. These reduce water loss and withstand harsh conditions, which helps them in cold and dry regions. Compare a needle with a broad leaf.

## Fertilisation Without Water

A fern needs a film of water so that its male cells can swim. Gymnosperms do not require water for fertilisation. A pine still needs water to live, and it carries that water in its xylem.
