## Plants of Cold, Dry Places

A fern needs water outside its body to reproduce. A pine does not, and pines are well suited to cold and dry regions. Look at where its seeds sit: out on the scales of a cone.

## Called Naked

Pines and cycads are gymnosperms, from the Greek gymnos, naked, and spermos, seed. Their seeds are not closed inside a fruit.
