## Flowers and Fruits

The gulmohar is an angiosperm, or flowering plant. The name comes from angeion, vessel, and spermos, seed, because its seeds are enclosed in a fruit. Angiosperms have the most complex body organisation of all plants.

<!-- STEP -->
## Pollen Moves First

Flowers attract pollinators, which carry pollen from flower to flower. This pollination makes reproduction more efficient. It happens before any seed exists, since a seed forms only afterwards.

<!-- STEP -->
## Seeds Move Later

A fruit forms around the seeds, and it helps spread them to new places. Insects, birds, other animals, wind and water can all carry seeds away. This is seed dispersal.

**Pollination moves pollen to a flower, and dispersal later moves seeds away.**

<!-- STEP -->
## The Most Diverse Group

Flowers and fruits allow angiosperms to live in a wide range of environments, so they are the most diverse plant group on the Earth. They still have a challenge: reproduction depends on pollination by different agents.
