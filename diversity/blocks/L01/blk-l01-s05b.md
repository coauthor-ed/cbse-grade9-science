## Two Conditions

A **biodiversity hotspot** is a region that meets two conditions: it supports a large number of endemic species, and it has undergone significant habitat loss. A long list of species proves neither.

<!-- STEP -->
## Hotspots in India

Four global hotspots reach India: the Western Ghats, Indo-Burma (including North East India), the Himalayas and Sundaland (including the Nicobar Islands). The shaded areas are approximate. Indo-Burma and Sundaland run far beyond India.

## Why One Country Holds So Much

India has mountains in the north, desert in the west, rainforests in the North East, plateaus in the south and long coastlines. Each region has its own soil and climate, so together these habitats support a wide variety of species.
