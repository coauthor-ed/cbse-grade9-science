## Field Sort: Two Rounds, One Set of Records

Six field records, spotted and written down. The letters are just labels, not real species.

| Record | Where observed | Body covering |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Air | Feathers |
| B | Air | Hair |
| C | Tree | Feathers |
| D | Tree | Hair |
| E | Air | Not observed |
| F | Unsure | Hair |

Sort them into crates - twice, by two different rules. Round 1 uses "where observed." Round 2 uses "body covering." Name what each crate holds, then place every record using only the evidence given.

## Let Someone Else Reproduce It

Some evidence won't answer a round's question yet - that record goes in the "still a mystery" crate, not a guessed one. Switch rounds anytime from the level tabs above the board, and change your crates freely.

**You win when both rounds check out: two valid groupings, and every gap still visibly unresolved instead of guessed.**

No timer, no penalty for trying again. Could someone else, given only your rule, land on exactly your crates?
