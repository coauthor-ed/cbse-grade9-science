## Found Only Here

Some species are restricted to one part of the world and are not found naturally anywhere else. These are **endemic** species. The Nilgiri tahr, a wild goat of the South Indian hills, and the lion-tailed macaque, a monkey with a grey mane, are two examples. A zoo elsewhere does not change this, because the word is about where a species lives naturally.

## Plants Too

Neelakurinji is a shrub of the South Indian hills that flowers about once in 12 years. *Nepenthes khasiana* is a pitcher plant of Meghalaya that traps insects in its leaves. Your book names these two as found only in India, like the tahr and the macaque.
