## More Than Many of One Kind

A thousand hens on one farm are many animals, but they are one species. **Biodiversity** is the variety of living organisms on the Earth, and that variety works through links between them:

- Plants capture sunlight and prepare food.
- Birds, bees and bats pollinate flowers.
- Fungi and bacteria decompose fallen leaves and make the soil fertile.
- Your book adds that microscopic algae in the oceans release most of the oxygen we breathe.

## One Word, Four Species

In Pakke, the word "hornbill" covers four species: the Rufous-necked, Oriental Pied, Great and Wreathed Hornbills. They look alike in some ways, yet each has its own head colour and bill, which is how scientists tell them apart.

## Why Each Lives Where It Does

Hornbills nest only in large, old trees with suitable cavities, and they feed on specific fruits. So different hornbill species are found in different parts of the forest, depending on tree size and fruit availability.

Suppose the large, old trees disappeared. The hornbills would have nowhere to nest, and the note "hornbills present" would give no warning.
