## One Forest, Nearly 300 Bird Species

The Pakke Tiger Reserve is a forest in Arunachal Pradesh. Scientists have recorded nearly 300 bird species there, which is striking, because India as a whole has about 1,300.

Four of those species are hornbills, large birds with huge curved bills. This one is the Great Hornbill.

## Two Teams, One Word

Imagine two survey teams visit Pakke in different years. This case is made up. Both teams write the same note: "hornbills present".

Now suppose the Rufous-necked Hornbill, seen here, vanished between the two visits. The other three hornbills would still be there, so the note would not change.
