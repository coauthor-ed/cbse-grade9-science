## Five Kinds of Land

Grouping is older than modern science in India. Sangam Tinai, from Tamil literature of about two thousand years ago, classifies landscapes into five kinds, each linked with its living things. Your book calls this a literary and cultural understanding of landscapes, not a formal ecological theory.
