## From a Heap to Shelves

With the books arranged by subject and author, you can walk straight to the right shelf. The Earth has the same problem on a larger scale, because it is home to millions of organisms.

Scientists group organisms by their similarities and differences. This scientific system of grouping is called **biological classification**.

<!-- STEP -->
## Six Uses

Biological classification helps us to:

- study living organisms in an organised way
- see similarities and differences
- understand how organisms are related and interact
- identify and name newly discovered organisms
- find the organisms under threat of extinction
- discuss organisms across the world in one common system
