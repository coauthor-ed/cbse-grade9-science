## One Pond, Many Kinds

A frog, a fish, a snail and a deer may all use the same pond. That shows they share a place. It does not show that they are close relatives.

## Small Differences Add Up

Where did so many forms of life come from? Small differences among individuals affected their chances of survival and reproduction, and those differences accumulated over many generations.

The picture shows a made-up bird that feeds on hard seeds. Press **Next generation** and watch the bills. No bird chooses its bill. Birds with thicker bills simply left more young in this case.

## One Line Becomes Two

Over a vast span of time, such changes gave rise to new forms of life. Forms that branched from one line keep many features they got from that line.

**Shared inherited features point to relatives. A shared place does not.**
