## Same Animals, New Groups

Under **Where seen** the eagle and the bat share the air. Under **Body covering** they part, because the eagle has feathers and the bat has fur. So the eagle and the bat do not stay together.

A **criterion** is the feature you choose for grouping. Your book's table groups the eagle, the tiger and the leopard as carnivores, using eating habits as the criterion.

**The same organism can fit into different groups, depending on the criterion you choose.**

So how do scientists decide which features to use? We will come to that after looking at what we are grouping.
