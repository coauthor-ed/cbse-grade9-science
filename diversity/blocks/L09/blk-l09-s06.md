## Fix the Tiger's Name

A student has written *panthera Tigris*. Both words are right, but the capital letter is on the wrong word. Press **Fix the name** to correct it.

## Fix the Mango's Name

A note gives the mango's name as *indica*. This is only the species name, and it cannot stand alone: the neem tree and the tamarind tree have *indica* as their second word too. Press **Add the genus**.

## Read a Label

Plants in gardens and parks often carry a label with several lines of information. This one is made up. Press the line that gives the scientific name.
