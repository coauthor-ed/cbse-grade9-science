## One Family for Both

The report cannot be right. The genus *Panthera* sits inside one family, Felidae, so the tiger and the lion both belong to it. Every lower group is a part of the group above it.

## An Address, From the Top

Classification goes step by step from very broad groups to smaller ones, and it works like an address. The tiger's address starts with kingdom Animalia, phylum Chordata and sub-phylum Vertebrata, an extra level, and then class Mammalia.

<!-- STEP -->
## Down to One Species

Inside Mammalia the address narrows: order Carnivora, family Felidae, genus *Panthera* and then the species *Panthera tigris*. A tiger stays a member of every larger group all the way down.
