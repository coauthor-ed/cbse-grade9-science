## One Animal, Four Names

A tiger is called *bagh* in Hindi, *puli* in Tamil, tiger in English and *tigre* in French. If people from different regions discuss this animal, confusion may arise.

## Too Broad to Help

A wildlife report says only "mammal". That is correct, but it could mean a tiger, a bat or a dolphin. So we need two things: an exact place among the groups of living things, and one name that everybody shares.
