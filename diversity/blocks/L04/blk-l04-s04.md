## Every Cell Touches the Water

Spirogyra is a green thread in pond water. Its cells sit end to end, and spiral green bands run through each one. It is an example of Thallophyta, the algae, which are among the simplest plant forms.

Its body is called a thallus. Water, nutrients and gases pass straight across the surface, so nothing inside it is far from its supplies.
