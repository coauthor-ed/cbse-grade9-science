## Look Inside the Stem

Ferns belong to Pteridophyta. They have true roots, stems and leaves. How could you test whether water and food travel through special tissues? Cut across a fern stem and look at it under a microscope, as drawn here.

<!-- STEP -->
## Xylem and Phloem

The cross section shows vascular tissues. Xylem carries water up from the roots. Phloem carries food made in the leaves to the other parts of the plant.

<!-- STEP -->
## Water Is Still Needed Outside

A fern still needs water to reproduce, and it makes no seeds. Its male cells swim through a thin film of water outside the body, as in moss, and xylem does nothing for them.

**Moving water inside a plant does not end its need for water outside.**
