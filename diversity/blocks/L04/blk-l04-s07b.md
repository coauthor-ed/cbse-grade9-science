## Your Turn: Look Closely

Try these in class with your teacher.

**Moss under a lens.** Observe a bryophyte with a hand lens and collect some in a watch glass. Put a drop of water on it and observe it under a dissecting microscope, which shows living specimens enlarged. How do its leaf-like parts differ from the leaves around your school?

**Two stems.** Recall the cross section of a sunflower stem from your book. Compare it with a cross section of a fern stem, then discuss the difference in class. These points may help:

- Where is the vascular tissue in each stem: in one central part, or in separate groups?
- Which tissue lies closer to the centre?
- How large is the vascular tissue compared with the whole stem?
