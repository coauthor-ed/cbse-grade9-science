## Two Plants, One Damp Wall

Moss and ferns can grow in the same damp places, and neither has flowers. Start with the moss: a low green mat of tiny shoots, with thin stalks standing up from it.

## Are They Built Alike?

A fern is larger, with leaves divided into many small parts. These are two separate photos, not drawn to one scale.

A photo cannot show how water moves inside a plant, or what a plant needs in order to reproduce. We will follow those two questions, starting with the simplest plants.
