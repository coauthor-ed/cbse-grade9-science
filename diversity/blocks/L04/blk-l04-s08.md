## Three Classes on the Tree

Thallophyta, Bryophyta and Pteridophyta are now on the tree. Spirogyra takes what it needs straight from the water. Moss holds on to damp ground. A fern carries water inside its body.

<!-- STEP -->
## One Need Remains

All three still depend on water to reproduce, and none of them makes seeds. These are groups living today, so moss did not turn into a fern.

The next lesson meets plants that reproduce without water outside, and asks what seeds and flowers change.
